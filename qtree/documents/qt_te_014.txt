Q29: In 3 sentences or less, explain the required data retention period for client records under our compliance policy.
A29: So the policy on this is actually pretty specific and worth walking through in a bit of detail. Client records aren't just kept indefinitely or tossed whenever someone feels like cleaning up a filing cabinet or server drive. Instead, the rule is that records need to stay on file for a full seven years, and that clock starts ticking from the moment the engagement with the client actually wraps up, not from when the file was first opened. This matters a lot for audits and any potential disputes down the line, so keeping track of engagement end dates is honestly just as important as keeping the records themselves.
