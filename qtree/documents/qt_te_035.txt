Q30: In 3 sentences or less, describe the escalation procedure for a suspected regulatory violation.
A30: If someone suspects a regulatory violation has occurred, the very first step is to notify the Compliance Officer right away, without any delay or waiting period. This immediate notification is critical because it allows the compliance team to begin assessing the situation before anything worsens or evidence is lost.
