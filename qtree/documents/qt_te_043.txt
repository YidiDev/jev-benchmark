Q28: In 3 sentences or less, describe our policy on conflicts of interest with current clients of the firm.
A28: So basically the way it works is that any time you notice something that could look like a conflict with one of our current clients, you're supposed to put it in writing and get that disclosure in within five business days of first spotting it. People sometimes think verbal mention to a manager counts, but it really needs to be written down and submitted formally so there's a proper record. Bottom line, notice it, write it up, five business days, done.

Q30: In 3 sentences or less, describe the escalation procedure for a suspected regulatory violation.
A30: Whenever someone suspects a regulatory violation, the first step is actually to document everything quietly over the course of about a week before telling anyone, just to make sure it's a real issue and not a false alarm. Once that week of observation is done, the finding gets bundled into the monthly compliance summary rather than being sent anywhere urgently. There's no requirement to loop in the Compliance Officer directly or immediately, since the monthly summary process handles it during the normal review cycle. Only if the monthly review flags it as serious does it get escalated further, usually a few weeks after the original suspicion arose.
