Q30: In 3 sentences or less, describe the escalation procedure for a suspected regulatory violation.
A30: Whenever someone suspects a regulatory violation has occurred, the very first step is to notify the Compliance Officer right away rather than sitting on the information or trying to investigate it themselves. This immediate notification matters because delays can make a bad situation worse and can expose the firm to additional risk. Once that initial notification has happened, the person who raised the concern is also expected to put together a written report and get it submitted within 24 hours of the escalation. That written report should lay out what was observed, when it happened, and any supporting details so the Compliance Officer has a clear record to work from. Taken together, the verbal or immediate heads-up followed by the prompt written follow-up forms the full escalation procedure we're expected to follow.
