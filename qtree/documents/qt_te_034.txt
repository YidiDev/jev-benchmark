Q29: In 3 sentences or less, explain the required data retention period for client records under our compliance policy.
A29: So, under our compliance policy, once an engagement with a client wraps up, we don't just delete or archive the files right away, there's actually a mandated retention window we have to observe. Specifically, the firm is required to hold onto client records for a full seven years measured from the date the engagement officially ends, not from when the file was created or last touched. This applies broadly to things like correspondence, contracts, financial documentation, and any other materials tied to that particular client relationship. The idea behind such a long window is to make sure records are available in case of audits, disputes, or regulatory inquiries that might come up well after the work itself is finished. So even though it might seem excessive to keep paperwork around for nearly a decade, that seven-year rule from engagement end is the standard we follow across the board.
