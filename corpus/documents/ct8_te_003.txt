This notice is issued pursuant to state administrative code governing employer withholding tax obligations and applies to the reporting period ending September 30, 2024.

General Information for Recipients

The Bureau of Corporate Revenue Compliance was established in 1974 to administer the collection and enforcement of state-level withholding, sales, and excise taxes on behalf of registered business entities operating within the state. The Bureau processes approximately 1.2 million filings annually and maintains regional processing offices in Columbus, Cleveland, and Cincinnati. Filings may be submitted electronically through the Bureau's online portal or by mail using the appropriate paper forms available on the Bureau's public website.

All information submitted to the Bureau in connection with tax filings, assessments, or appeals is treated as confidential business information and is protected from public disclosure except as required by law or court order. Bureau employees are bound by confidentiality agreements and are subject to disciplinary action, including termination, for unauthorized disclosure of taxpayer information. Taxpayers may request copies of their own filing history at any time by submitting a written request to the Records Division.

Assessment Details

Upon review of quarterly withholding return Form WH-3 submitted by Halloway & Sons Distribution LLC for the period ending September 30, 2024, the Bureau has identified a filing deficiency. The amount of state income tax withheld from employee wages, as reported, was $52,110.40. However, based on payroll records submitted separately through the state's wage reporting system, the correct withholding amount should have been $58,940.15. This results in an underpayment of $6,829.75.

Additional Program Information

The Bureau also administers several taxpayer assistance programs, including a payment plan option for businesses experiencing temporary cash flow difficulties, and an annual tax literacy workshop series held each spring in partnership with local chambers of commerce. Businesses interested in these programs may contact the Bureau's Taxpayer Assistance Line for more information. The Bureau additionally publishes a quarterly newsletter summarizing recent changes to withholding regulations, available by subscription at no cost.

Penalty and Interest Calculation

Pursuant to state code section 5747.13, a penalty of 10 percent of the underpayment amount has been assessed, totaling $682.98. Interest accrues on the unpaid balance at a rate of 6 percent per annum, calculated daily from the original due date of October 31, 2024. As of the date of this notice, accrued interest totals $28.44. Total amount due, including tax, penalty, and interest, is $7,541.17.

Payment Instructions

Payment must be received by the Bureau no later than December 15, 2024, to avoid further penalty accrual. Payments may be submitted electronically through the Bureau's online portal referenced above, or by certified check made payable to the Bureau of Corporate Revenue Compliance, mailed to the address listed at the top of this notice.

Appeal Rights

Halloway & Sons Distribution LLC has the right to appeal this assessment within 60 days of the date of this notice by filing a written protest with the Bureau's Appeals Division, including supporting documentation for the disputed amounts.
