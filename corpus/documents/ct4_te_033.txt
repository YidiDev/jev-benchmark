To: Facilities and Procurement Staff
From: Denise Halloway, Contracts Administration
Date: November 6, 2023
Re: Status of Cleaning Services Agreement with Briarwood Maintenance Co.

This memo is to clarify the current status of the Cleaning Services Agreement executed with Briarwood Maintenance Co. on January 12, 2022, covering janitorial services at the Northgate office complex.

Please be advised that this Agreement was superseded by the Amended and Restated Cleaning Services Agreement dated August 30, 2023. The amended agreement adjusts the monthly service fee, extends coverage to the third-floor conference suites, and updates the termination notice period from thirty to sixty days.

Staff should no longer refer to the January 2022 agreement when processing invoices, scheduling service requests, or evaluating vendor performance. All references, including in purchase orders and internal tracking sheets, should be updated to cite the August 30, 2023 Amended and Restated Agreement going forward.

If you locate any documents or systems still referencing the original January 2022 agreement, please forward them to Contracts Administration so they can be corrected. Questions may be directed to my office.
