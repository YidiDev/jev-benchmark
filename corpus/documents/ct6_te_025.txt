INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: All Regional Sales Managers
From: Legal and Contracts Department
Date: September 5, 2024
Re: Vendor Code of Conduct Acknowledgment Requirement

This memo confirms the Vendor Code of Conduct Acknowledgment requirement now in effect as of September 5, 2024. All regional sales managers must ensure that vendors engaged for events, promotional materials, or third-party services execute the attached acknowledgment form prior to the start of any new engagement or contract renewal.

The acknowledgment covers anti-bribery provisions, data handling standards, and conflict-of-interest disclosures applicable to vendors interacting with company representatives. Signed forms should be uploaded to the vendor management portal under the corresponding contract file.

A related version of this document has an effective date of December 19, 2022. Managers reviewing older vendor files or historical contracts should note which version was in place at the time the vendor relationship was established, as acknowledgment language may differ between versions.

Please route any completed forms to Legal by the end of each quarter for compliance tracking. Direct questions to the Contracts team distribution list.
