TO: Marcus Whitfield, Director of Operations
FROM: Elena Sokolova, Procurement Manager
DATE: March 14, 2024
RE: Execution of Service Agreement with Brightline Facilities Group

This memo confirms that the Master Service Agreement between Caldwell Logistics Inc. and Brightline Facilities Group ("Vendor") was signed by both parties on March 12, 2024, and is now in effect.

Under the terms of the agreement, Brightline Facilities Group will provide janitorial and grounds maintenance services at our Newark distribution center for a period of twelve months, beginning April 1, 2024, and terminating March 31, 2025. Caldwell Logistics Inc. agrees to pay a fixed monthly fee of $8,750, invoiced on the first business day of each month, with payment due within 30 days of receipt.

Brightline Facilities Group is obligated to maintain general liability insurance of no less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and to provide a dedicated on-site supervisor during all business hours. Either party may terminate the agreement with 60 days' written notice.

Please ensure accounts payable is updated with the new vendor record and payment schedule. Contact me with any questions regarding the terms.
