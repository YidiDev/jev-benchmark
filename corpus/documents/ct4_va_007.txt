INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: Finance and Procurement Team
From: Denise Callahan, Contracts Administrator
Date: April 3, 2024
Re: Status Update – Warehouse Logistics Services Agreement with Tanager Freight Solutions

This memo is to formally update our records regarding the Warehouse Logistics Services Agreement originally executed with Tanager Freight Solutions on June 15, 2020 (Contract No. WLS-2020-118). Under that Agreement, Tanager Freight Solutions provided pallet storage, order fulfillment, and third-party shipping coordination services for our Midwest distribution operations.

Please be advised that this Agreement was superseded by the Amended and Restated Warehouse Logistics Services Agreement dated October 8, 2023, which reflects updated storage fee schedules, revised liability caps, and an expanded service territory covering our new Indianapolis facility.

Going forward, all invoicing, service level questions, and correspondence with Tanager Freight Solutions should reference the October 8, 2023 Amended and Restated Agreement exclusively. The original June 15, 2020 agreement should no longer be cited in any active procurement or billing documentation.

Please update your files accordingly and direct any questions to my office.
