Internal Filing No. HR-2022-118

Subject: Expense Reporting and Reimbursement Procedure

This notice documents the Expense Reporting and Reimbursement Procedure maintained by the Finance Department, effective October 19, 2022. The procedure establishes the standard workflow for submitting, reviewing, and approving employee expense reports, including required documentation thresholds and reimbursement timelines.

Under this procedure, all expense reports must be submitted through the Concur system within 15 business days of the expense being incurred. Reports totaling more than $1,000 require secondary approval from a cost center manager prior to processing. Mileage reimbursement rates are set quarterly by the Finance Department and published on the internal finance portal.

A related version of this document has an effective date of November 3, 2022. Staff preparing expense reports should confirm with their department's finance liaison which version applies to their submission cycle.

This filing is maintained in the Finance Department's internal policy archive and is available upon request to any employee. Questions regarding specific line items or approval routing should be directed to the Accounts Payable helpdesk at extension 4471.
