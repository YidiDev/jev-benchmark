ADMINISTRATIVE NOTICE — BUSINESS TRAVEL POLICY

Issued by: Corporate Administration, Halden Ridge Manufacturing Inc.
Effective Date: March 20, 2025
Document Reference: CA-TR-2025-03

This notice establishes updated procedures governing domestic and international business travel for employees of Halden Ridge Manufacturing Inc. Covered topics include pre-approval requirements for trips exceeding $1,500 in estimated cost, permissible booking channels, per diem rates by destination tier, and documentation requirements for reimbursement submissions through the internal Concur portal.

All department heads are responsible for reviewing travel requests submitted by their staff and confirming that the requested itinerary aligns with budgetary allocations for the fiscal quarter. Requests lacking proper documentation will be returned to the submitting employee for correction prior to processing.

A related version of this document has an effective date of September 17, 2024. Staff preparing travel authorizations should verify the applicable version by consulting the document reference number printed at the top of each form, as procedures referenced in supporting materials may correspond to either date.

Inquiries regarding this notice may be directed to the Corporate Administration office, extension 4471.
