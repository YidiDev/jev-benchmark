Turning now to the primary purpose of this letter: we have received a Notice of Withholding Assessment from the Franchise Tax Board dated February 20, 2025, concerning the Castellano Family Trust's 2023 state tax filing. The notice indicates a discrepancy in reported withholding of $4,318.00 related to distributions made from the trust during the third quarter of 2023, and it requests either payment of the assessed amount or a written response with supporting documentation within 30 days of the notice date. Given the mailing date, this places the response deadline at approximately March 22, 2025.

As part of our standard practice, I want to remind you that all correspondence regarding your account, including this letter, is treated as strictly confidential and is intended solely for your review and the review of any authorized representatives you designate in writing. We do not share client financial information with third parties except as required by law or with your explicit consent.

Returning to the assessment itself, our review of the trust's records suggests the discrepancy likely arose from a timing mismatch between the trustee's quarterly distribution reports and the Franchise Tax Board's own reconciliation records, rather than an actual underpayment. We have compiled the relevant distribution statements and withholding records from that period and intend to submit a formal response on the trust's behalf, disputing the assessed amount, no later than March 17, 2025, to allow a buffer before the deadline.
