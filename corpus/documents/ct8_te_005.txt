Dear Mr. Callahan,

I hope this letter finds you well. I'm writing on behalf of Brightland & Moss Advisory Partners to follow up on a matter our team has been reviewing on your behalf, as well as to share a few general updates regarding our firm that we like to include periodically for our long-standing clients.

As you may recall, Brightland & Moss was founded in 1976 by our namesake partners, Robert Brightland and Susan Moss, who met while working together at a regional accounting firm in Richmond, Virginia. What began as a two-person tax preparation practice has grown into a full-service advisory firm with offices in Charlotte, Raleigh, and Columbia, serving clients primarily in the textile, furniture, and light manufacturing sectors. We remain proud of our roots and our continued focus on personalized service, even as our client roster has expanded to include several publicly traded companies. We mention this from time to time simply because so many of our newer client contacts have never heard the full history, and we think it's a nice reminder of the values that continue to guide our work.

Turning to the matter at hand: as part of our ongoing review of Dunmore Textile Holdings' state tax compliance, we were copied on correspondence received from the North Carolina Department of Revenue dated September 25, 2024. The correspondence constitutes a formal tax assessment notice concerning your company's 2022 corporate income tax return, specifically addressing an adjustment to reported net taxable income following the Department's audit of intercompany transactions between Dunmore Textile Holdings and its Georgia-based subsidiary. The Department's notice proposes an additional tax liability of $52,340.00, along with interest of $6,118.75 calculated through the date of the notice, for a combined total of $58,458.75. The notice further states that Dunmore has 30 days from the date of issuance — meaning a response deadline of October 25, 2024 — to either remit payment or file a formal request for administrative review with supporting documentation.

Before proceeding further, I want to include our standard confidentiality notice, as this letter references sensitive financial matters specific to your company: this correspondence is intended solely for the addressee named above and is confidential in nature. It should not be shared with parties outside your organization's finance and legal departments without prior written consent from Brightland & Moss Advisory Partners. If you believe you have received this letter in error, please contact our office immediately so that we may arrange for its proper handling.

Given the relatively short window before the response deadline, our recommendation is that we schedule a call this week to discuss whether to pursue an administrative review of the assessment, particularly regarding the intercompany pricing methodology the Department appears to be challenging. Our tax controversy team, led by Marguerite Feldman, has reviewed the underlying transfer pricing study prepared in 2022 and believes there are reasonable grounds to contest at least a portion of the proposed adjustment.

As always, we appreciate the continued trust you and your team place in Brightland & Moss, and we remain committed to providing the same level of attentive, relationship-driven service that has defined our firm since 1976. Please let us know a convenient time this week to discuss next steps regarding the North Carolina notice, as the deadline is approaching quickly.

Warm regards,
