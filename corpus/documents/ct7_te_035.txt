STATUS FILING — INTERNAL RECORD

Reporting Period: November 4–18, 2024
Filed by the Talon team

This filing documents progress on the archive digitization project currently underway at the regional records center.

To date, the team has completed scanning and indexing of approximately 14,300 physical files out of an estimated 38,000 total, placing the project slightly ahead of the pace projected in the October planning memo. Quality control review of a sample batch (n=500) returned an error rate of 1.2%, within acceptable tolerance.

Two scanners experienced mechanical failure during this period; one has been repaired and returned to service, while the second is awaiting a replacement part expected to arrive November 25. This is not anticipated to significantly affect the overall timeline, though the team requests approval to extend temporary staffing through December 6 to compensate for lost scanning hours.

Storage capacity on the primary digital archive server remains at 61% utilization, with no immediate concerns regarding available space.

Submitted on behalf of Team Talon, this record will be followed by the next scheduled filing on December 2, 2024.
