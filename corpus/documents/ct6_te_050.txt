NOTICE OF INTERNAL POLICY — REMOTE WORK EQUIPMENT STIPEND

Policy Reference: HR-RW-114
Effective Date: June 3, 2025

This notice sets forth the terms governing the Remote Work Equipment Stipend Program administered by the Human Resources Department. Under this policy, eligible employees who perform at least 60% of their scheduled work hours from a non-company location may submit reimbursement requests for approved home office equipment, up to a maximum of $500 per fiscal year.

Reimbursement requests must be submitted through the internal expense portal within 30 days of purchase and must include an itemized receipt. Approved categories include ergonomic seating, monitors, keyboards, and dedicated internet routers. Items not listed require pre-approval from a department manager.

A related version of this document has an effective date of August 25, 2025. Employees and managers referencing this policy should confirm which version applies to the relevant time period before processing reimbursement requests.

Questions regarding eligibility or the submission process may be directed to the Human Resources shared mailbox. This notice will remain posted on the internal policy repository for employee reference.
