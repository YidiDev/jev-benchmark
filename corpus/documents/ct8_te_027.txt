Northgate Fabrication & Millwork Co. was established in 1978 as a small custom cabinetry shop serving residential contractors in the Dayton metropolitan area. Over the following four decades, the company expanded its operations to include commercial millwork, architectural paneling, and custom fixture assembly for retail chains throughout the Midwest. Today, Northgate operates two production facilities totaling over 95,000 square feet and employs approximately 140 skilled tradespeople, including certified cabinetmakers, finishers, and CNC machine operators. The company remains family-owned, currently under the leadership of third-generation president Marcus Halloway.
