Over the past three decades, Coleridge Freight has grown into a mid-sized regional carrier with a fleet of 62 tractors and 90 trailers, serving clients across Oklahoma, Kansas, and northern Texas. We have maintained a consistently positive working relationship with their dispatch team, and their on-time delivery rate over the past twelve months has exceeded 97 percent according to our internal logistics tracking dashboard.
