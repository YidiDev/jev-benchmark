INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: All Department Heads
From: Renata Osei, Office of Corporate Compliance
Date: April 20, 2022
Re: Conflict of Interest Disclosure Procedure

This memo issues the Conflict of Interest Disclosure Procedure, effective April 20, 2022. This procedure requires all employees at the manager level and above to complete an annual disclosure form identifying any outside business interests, board memberships, or family relationships that could reasonably present a conflict with company duties.

Disclosure forms must be submitted through the Compliance Office's online portal no later than June 30 of each calendar year. New hires at qualifying levels must submit an initial disclosure within 30 days of their start date. The Compliance Office will review all submissions and follow up directly with employees whose disclosures require further clarification.

A related version of this document has an effective date of March 22, 2022. Department heads should confirm with the Compliance Office which version applies when advising staff on current disclosure obligations.

Please distribute this memo to all managers within your department and confirm receipt to the Compliance Office by the end of the week.

Renata Osei
Office of Corporate Compliance
