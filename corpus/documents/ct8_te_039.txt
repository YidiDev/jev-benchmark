Dear Mr. Aylward,

Thank you for the productive discussions our teams have had over the past two months regarding a potential working relationship between Whitfield & Okafor Business Solutions and Kestrel Print & Media Co. I'm pleased to write to you today to confirm the finalized terms of our agreement.

Before addressing the specifics, I'd like to share a bit about our firm, particularly for the benefit of anyone on your team who may not be familiar with our history. Whitfield & Okafor was founded in 1987 by Harold Whitfield and Ngozi Okafor as a small bookkeeping practice serving manufacturing clients in central Ohio. Over the following three decades, the firm expanded into full-service business consulting, including operations support, payroll administration, and vendor management services. We now employ 62 people across two offices and have worked with over 300 clients throughout the Midwest. We remain, at heart, a firm built on long-term relationships rather than transactional engagements, which is part of why we're glad to formalize things with Kestrel in writing.

As is standard in our correspondence, I want to note that any information exchanged between our organizations, including the terms described in this letter, should be treated as confidential business information and not disclosed to third parties without written consent from both parties.

Turning now to the agreement itself: Whitfield & Okafor Business Solutions and Kestrel Print & Media Co. have reached a service agreement effective July 1st, under which our firm will provide monthly bookkeeping, accounts payable processing, and payroll administration services for Kestrel's 34-employee operation. This letter, once countersigned below, constitutes execution of that agreement. The agreed monthly fee for these services is $6,200, payable by the 10th of each month via ACH transfer, with the first payment due July 10th. The initial term of the agreement is 12 months, with automatic renewal for successive 12-month terms unless either party provides written notice of non-renewal at least 45 days prior to the renewal date.

I should also mention, purely as an aside, that our office will be relocating within the same building to Suite 410 in August due to a lease renegotiation with the property owner — this shouldn't affect any of our services or points of contact, but I wanted to flag it in case you try to visit in person during that transition.

Returning to the terms of our engagement: Whitfield & Okafor agrees to deliver monthly financial statements no later than the 8th business day following month-end, maintain strict data security protocols for all payroll and financial information, and designate a dedicated account manager — in this case, Ms. Renata Silva — as Kestrel's primary point of contact. In turn, Kestrel Print & Media Co. agrees to provide timely access to relevant financial records, designate an internal liaison for approvals, and remit payment according to the schedule outlined above. Either party may terminate this agreement for cause with 30 days' written notice if the other party fails to cure a material breach within that period.

For your records, I've included as an appendix a general overview of our firm's service tiers and a brief history of our involvement with the Central Ohio Small Business Alliance, which some of your colleagues may find of interest given Kestrel's own membership in that organization.

Please sign and return one copy of this letter to indicate your acceptance of these terms. We look forward to a strong working relationship.
