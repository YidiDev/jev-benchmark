To: All Staff, Finance and Accounting
From: Marcus Whitfield, Controller
Date: May 29, 2023
Re: Expense Reimbursement Procedure

This memo communicates the Expense Reimbursement Procedure effective May 29, 2023, applicable to all employees submitting business-related expense claims through the shared services portal.

Under this procedure, employees must submit itemized receipts for any single expense exceeding $25, and reports must be filed within 30 calendar days of the expense being incurred. Approvals require sign-off from the employee's direct supervisor and, for amounts over $1,000, from the relevant department head. Mileage reimbursement continues to follow the standard rate published annually by Finance.

For reference, a related version of this document has an effective date of March 16, 2022. Staff working across multiple teams or transitioning between departments should confirm with their supervisor which version governs their current submissions.

All reimbursement requests should continue to be routed through the standard portal. Questions about specific line items, receipt thresholds, or approval routing can be directed to the Accounts Payable helpdesk. This memo should be retained alongside other departmental finance procedures.
