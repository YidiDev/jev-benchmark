NOTICE OF TAX ASSESSMENT

Issuing Authority: Ohio Department of Taxation
Taxpayer: Blackwood Industrial Supply LLC
Taxpayer ID: 31-0987456
Notice Number: OH-ASMT-20451
Date of Issue: February 14, 2024
Tax Period: Calendar Year 2022

Based on a review of filed returns and third-party wage reports, the Department has determined an underpayment of state withholding tax in the amount of $9,842.17, plus accrued interest of $612.30, for a total assessed balance of $10,454.47.

This assessment results from a discrepancy between Form IT-941 filings submitted by the taxpayer and quarterly wage statements reported by employer Blackwood Industrial Supply LLC for the periods ending March 31, June 30, September 30, and December 31, 2022.

Payment in full is due within thirty (30) days of the date of this notice. Taxpayers disputing this assessment may file a written protest with the Department's Appeals Division no later than March 15, 2024, including supporting documentation.

Failure to respond within the stated period will result in referral to collections and potential lien filing against business assets.
