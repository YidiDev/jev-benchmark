NOTICE OF CONTRACT STATUS
Reference: Vendor Services Agreement No. VSA-2021-118
Parties: Kestrel Facilities Group, Inc. and Marrow & Doyle Logistics LLC

This notice is filed for administrative record purposes regarding the above-referenced Vendor Services Agreement, originally executed on March 4, 2021, for the provision of warehouse maintenance and janitorial services at the Elmhurst Distribution Center.

This Agreement was superseded by the Amended and Restated Vendor Services Agreement dated September 19, 2023, which incorporates revised pricing schedules, updated service level requirements, and an expanded scope covering the Fairview Annex facility. Effective as of the September 19, 2023 date, the original Vendor Services Agreement No. VSA-2021-118 is no longer operative and shall not be relied upon for purposes of billing, service standards, or dispute resolution.

All future correspondence, invoicing, and performance evaluations related to warehouse maintenance and janitorial services should reference the Amended and Restated Vendor Services Agreement dated September 19, 2023. Copies of the amended agreement are available through the Vendor Management office upon request.
