Subject: FYI - Upcoming filing plus some general updates

Hi all,

Hope everyone's week is going well. I wanted to send along a quick note that covers a few things, including an important tax matter, so please read through even though there's some general company stuff mixed in here too.

First, a bit of background for anyone newer to the team: Caldwell & Voss Textiles was founded in 1987 by brothers Arthur and Neil Caldwell, originally as a small weaving operation out of a converted warehouse in Fall River, Massachusetts. Over the decades we've grown into a mid-sized supplier of industrial fabric goods, serving clients across the Northeast and Midwest. We now employ roughly 340 people across three facilities, and we take a lot of pride in our history of steady, sustainable growth. Our company picnic is coming up on the 22nd, by the way, and sign-ups are in the break room if anyone wants to bring a dish.

Now, onto the main item. Our accounting department has received a Notice of Tax Assessment from the Massachusetts Department of Revenue, dated September 18, 2024, regarding underreported withholding for the 2023 tax year. The notice indicates an assessed additional liability of $18,742.60, covering shortfalls in state withholding remittances for the third and fourth quarters of 2023. The Department has requested either payment in full or a formal response disputing the assessment within 30 days of the notice date, which puts our deadline at October 18, 2024.

Just so everyone understands the context: this appears to stem from a payroll system transition we underwent last spring, when we moved from the old ledger-based system to the new cloud payroll platform. During that transition, a subset of withholding calculations for hourly employees in the Fall River and Providence locations were miscalculated for two consecutive quarters before the error was caught and corrected internally in January 2024. Finance believes this assessment corresponds to that known discrepancy, though they are still reconciling the exact figures against our internal records.

As a reminder to everyone, please treat any correspondence regarding this matter as confidential. Per our standard internal communications policy, financial and regulatory correspondence should not be forwarded outside the finance and executive teams without prior approval. This is standard boilerplate we include in most financial emails, so don't read too much into it, but it does apply here given the sensitivity of tax matters.

Also, a general reminder that open enrollment for benefits starts November 1st this year, a bit earlier than usual, so keep an eye out for that packet from HR. And if anyone hasn't turned in their expense reports from the trade show in Chicago last month, please get those in by Friday so accounting can close out the month cleanly.

Back to the assessment: Denise in Finance is drafting a formal response to the Department of Revenue, and legal counsel (Whitfield & Marsh, our outside firm) has been looped in to review the reconciliation figures before anything is submitted. We expect to have a draft response ready for internal review by October 10, with final submission no later than October 17, one day ahead of the deadline.

Lastly, just a general FYI that our annual company history slideshow, the one we show new hires during orientation, has been updated this year to include photos from the Providence facility expansion in 2021. If anyone has old photos from the early days of the company they'd like to contribute, please send them to Marketing.

Let me know if you have questions about any of the above, especially the assessment notice, since that's the piece with an actual deadline attached.
