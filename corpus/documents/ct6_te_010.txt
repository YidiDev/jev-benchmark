To: Regional Operations Managers
From: Carlos Mendez, VP of Operations
Date: August 16, 2024
Re: Facility Access Badge Issuance Policy

This memo documents the policy for issuance and deactivation of employee access badges across all regional facilities. This version of the policy is effective August 16, 2024. Under the policy, badge requests must originate from an employee's direct manager and be submitted at least five business days prior to the requested activation date. Security desk personnel are responsible for verifying photo identification against HR records before issuing any badge.

For reference, a related version of this document has an effective date of November 27, 2022. Facilities staff maintaining archived copies of this policy should note both dates when responding to audit inquiries or historical access reviews, as the badge deactivation timelines described in each version are not identical.

Managers with questions about current badge request procedures should contact the Regional Security Office directly. This memo should be distributed to all site security coordinators and retained in the facility operations binder.
