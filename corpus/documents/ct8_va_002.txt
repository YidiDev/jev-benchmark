We hope this letter finds you and your team at Braxton & Pell Millworks, Inc. in good health and good spirits as the first quarter draws to a close. Meridian Falls Revenue & Tax Advisory Group has had the privilege of serving manufacturing and industrial clients throughout the Halden City metropolitan corridor for nearly thirty-one years, and we remain grateful for the trust your organization has placed in our firm since our engagement began in 2016. Founded in 1993 by the late Constance Meridian and her business partner Oswald Falls, our firm began as a two-person bookkeeping practice operating out of a converted storefront on Delacroix Avenue before growing into the regional advisory presence we are today, with three offices and a staff of forty-two credentialed professionals.

This letter serves as formal notification of the results of our review of Braxton & Pell Millworks, Inc.'s federal and state withholding obligations for the 2023 tax year. Based on our reconciliation of payroll records submitted through your third-party processor, ClearLedger Payroll Services, we have determined that your organization under-remitted federal income tax withholding in the amount of $18,340.27 for the period covering the third and fourth quarters of 2023. Additionally, our review identified a shortfall of $2,915.60 in Ohio state withholding for the same period, attributable to a misclassification of eleven seasonal employees as independent contractors rather than statutory employees. These amounts, together with applicable penalties and interest calculated through the date of this letter, bring the total assessed liability to $24,662.91, payable to the Ohio Department of Taxation and the Internal Revenue Service according to the enclosed remittance schedule.

Speaking more broadly of our firm's history, Meridian Falls has weathered several notable periods of transition, including our merger with the smaller Whitcombe Tax Partners in 2004, which brought us our current specialization in manufacturing sector compliance. We take considerable pride in our longstanding relationships with clients such as yours, many of whom have remained with us for over a decade. Our annual client appreciation dinner, held each October at the Halden City Riverside Pavilion, remains one of the highlights of our office calendar, and we hope to see representatives from Braxton & Pell Millworks in attendance again this year.

Please arrange for payment of the assessed amount, or submission of a formal dispute in writing, within thirty (30) days of the date of this letter. Failure to respond within this window may result in additional penalties accruing at a rate of 0.5% per month on the outstanding balance, as provided under applicable state and federal regulations. Our office is available to discuss a payment plan or to review supporting documentation should you wish to contest any portion of this assessment.

We also want to note, purely as a courtesy, that our office will be relocating within the same building to Suite 1140 effective June 1, 2024, though all phone numbers, fax lines, and mailing addresses will remain unchanged during this transition.

Should you have any questions regarding this notice or wish to schedule a consultation to discuss remediation steps, please do not hesitate to contact our office directly.
