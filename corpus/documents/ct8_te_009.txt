This letter serves as formal notice regarding the federal income tax withholding assessment for Brightline Textiles Inc. for the third quarter of fiscal year 2024. Based on our review of payroll disbursements submitted through your ADP integration, we have determined that total withholding remitted to the Internal Revenue Service for the period ending September 30, 2024, was underreported by $14,320.75. This discrepancy appears to stem from a miscalculation in supplemental wage withholding applied to bonus payments issued in August. We recommend that Brightline Textiles submit an amended Form 941 no later than November 15, 2024, along with payment of the outstanding balance, to avoid additional penalty accrual under IRS guidelines.

As a firm, Meridian has always emphasized education alongside compliance. Returning to the matter of your withholding assessment: interest on the underpayment, calculated at the applicable federal short-term rate plus three percent, currently totals $412.18 as of the date of this letter and will continue to accrue daily until the balance is paid in full.
