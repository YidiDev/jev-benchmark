INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: Region 4 Payroll Supervisors
From: Teresa Lindqvist, Director of Tax Compliance
Date: January 22, 2024
Re: 2023 Fourth Quarter Withholding Assessment Notice from State Revenue Office

This memo is to inform all payroll supervisors that Carrow Industrial Supply Inc. has received a formal tax assessment notice from the Ohio Department of Taxation regarding our fourth-quarter 2023 state withholding filings.

The assessment, dated January 18, 2024, cites a discrepancy of $9,840.60 between the withholding amounts remitted and the amounts reported on employee W-2 forms for the Springfield distribution facility. An additional penalty of $625.00 has been applied for late reconciliation, bringing the total balance owed to $10,465.60.

Payroll teams are asked to pull all Q4 2023 payroll registers for the Springfield location and cross-check them against the submitted remittance records by February 5. Finance has confirmed the payment is due to the state by February 20, 2024, to avoid further interest charges.

Please route any documentation discrepancies directly to my office. We will be filing an amended reconciliation statement once the review is complete.

Teresa
