TO: Regional Operations Managers, Blackwell Transit Solutions
FROM: Marguerite Feldstein, VP of Supply Chain Contracts
DATE: September 9, 2024
RE: Finalized Fleet Maintenance Agreement with Corrigan Diesel Services

This memo serves to formally notify regional managers of a newly executed vendor agreement and to provide relevant background for those coordinating fleet scheduling going forward.

By way of introduction for newer staff, Blackwell Transit Solutions has provided regional freight transport services across the tri-state area since 1994. Our fleet has grown from an initial eight trucks operating out of a single depot to a current roster of 142 vehicles distributed across six regional depots. This growth has necessarily increased the complexity of our maintenance operations, and leadership has periodically revisited whether maintenance should be handled in-house or through external contractors. This background is offered only for context and does not itself reflect any change in company policy.

Before discussing the agreement itself, please note our standard confidentiality reminder: any pricing terms, service schedules, or vendor performance data referenced in this memo are considered proprietary business information. Distribution of this memo or its contents outside of Blackwell Transit Solutions management is prohibited without prior approval from the Legal department. This applies equally to conversations with competing carriers or with journalists covering the regional trucking industry, a topic that has received some press attention in recent months following fuel price fluctuations.

Turning to the matter at hand: effective October 1, 2024, Blackwell Transit Solutions has entered into a signed three-year Fleet Maintenance and Repair Services Agreement with Corrigan Diesel Services, a diesel mechanics firm based in Toledo, Ohio. The agreement, executed by both parties on August 29, 2024, obligates Corrigan Diesel Services to provide scheduled preventative maintenance, emergency roadside repair dispatch, and annual DOT inspection support for all vehicles at the Toledo and Lima depots. The contract establishes a fixed annual service fee of $268,500, plus a per-incident emergency repair rate not to exceed $185 per hour of labor, billed monthly against actual usage. Corrigan Diesel Services is further obligated to maintain a response time of no more than ninety minutes for emergency roadside calls within a fifty-mile radius of either depot, and to carry commercial liability insurance of at least $3,000,000. In exchange, Blackwell Transit Solutions is obligated to provide advance quarterly maintenance schedules, secure bay access at both depots, and payment within thirty days of invoice receipt.

For general reference, our procurement team reviewed proposals from three vendors, including the incumbent contractor, before selecting Corrigan Diesel Services based on pricing, response-time guarantees, and favorable references from two comparable regional carriers in Indiana and Michigan. It may also interest managers to know that Corrigan Diesel Services has been in operation since 2003 and specializes primarily in heavy-duty diesel fleets, a specialization that aligns well with our vehicle composition.

As an appendix, attached to this memo is a general summary of current fuel price trends across our operating region, compiled by our finance team for unrelated budgeting purposes. This appendix has no bearing on the terms of the Corrigan Diesel Services agreement and is included solely as supplementary reading for managers interested in broader cost trends affecting depot operations.

Regional managers at the Toledo and Lima depots should coordinate directly with Corrigan Diesel Services' scheduling office to establish the first quarterly maintenance calendar under the new agreement. Questions regarding contract terms should be directed to my office, as I signed the agreement on behalf of Blackwell Transit Solutions.

Marguerite Feldstein
VP of Supply Chain Contracts
Blackwell Transit Solutions
