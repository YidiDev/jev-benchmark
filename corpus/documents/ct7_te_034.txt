TO: Site Directors
FROM: Beatrice Long
DATE: November 8
RE: Bi-Weekly Status Report

This report is filed by the Osprey team covering the period of October 26 through November 8.

Progress on the facility upgrade continues on schedule. Installation of the new ventilation units in Building C was completed on November 5, three days ahead of the original timeline. Contractor invoices for this phase have been submitted to accounting for processing.

The internal survey on employee workspace satisfaction closed on November 6 with a 74% response rate. Preliminary results indicate general satisfaction with the new seating arrangements, though several respondents noted ongoing concerns about lighting in the east wing, which we plan to address in the next phase of upgrades.

Budget tracking remains within approved limits, with roughly 62% of the allocated funds spent to date against a project timeline that is 55% complete, indicating we are slightly ahead of pace.

One outstanding item: the delivery of replacement office furniture has been delayed until November 18 due to a supplier backlog. This is not expected to impact the overall project timeline.

Our next report will be filed on November 22.

Beatrice Long
On behalf of the Osprey team
