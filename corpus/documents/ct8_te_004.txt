TO: Regional Finance Managers
FROM: Priya Anand, Director of Corporate Accounting
DATE: November 4, 2024
RE: Quarterly Roundup and Follow-Up Items

This memo covers several administrative matters that have accumulated over the past few weeks. Please read through in full, as a few items require action before month-end.

To begin, a brief note on where things stand with the Northgate office expansion: construction is now roughly 70% complete, and the leasing team expects the new space to be ready for occupancy by mid-January 2025. This has been a long time coming — we first identified the need for additional space back in 2021 when the Denver team grew past capacity in our existing lease. I know several of you have asked about desk assignments; those will be communicated separately by facilities once the space is finalized.

For those newer to the company, a quick refresher: Anand & Whitcomb Financial Group was established in 1998 by two former audit partners who wanted to build a firm focused on mid-market manufacturing clients. We've since grown to serve clients across twelve states, with a particular concentration in the Midwest and Mountain West regions. Our core values — integrity, responsiveness, and technical excellence — have remained consistent since the firm's founding, even as our headcount has grown from four employees to just over 340 today.

Moving to a more time-sensitive matter: our team received a withholding tax assessment notice from the Colorado Department of Revenue on October 22, 2024, concerning the 2023 fourth-quarter filing period for our Denver payroll office. The notice indicates that the state's records show a shortfall between the amount withheld from employee wages and the amount actually remitted to the state, specifically a difference of $7,215.30, plus statutory interest of $312.85 accrued through the notice date, bringing the total balance to $7,528.15. The Department has requested either payment in full or a written explanation of discrepancy within 20 business days of the notice date, which puts our deadline at approximately November 19, 2024. Payroll operations has already begun reconciling the underlying wage reports and believes the discrepancy stems from a timing issue related to the mid-year transition to our new payroll processor, ClearPath Systems, which went live on July 1, 2023.

As a standard reminder given the sensitive financial details discussed above, this memo and any attachments are intended solely for internal distribution among Regional Finance Managers and related accounting staff. Please do not forward outside the department without prior approval, and please dispose of printed copies securely once reviewed. This is boilerplate language we include on most memos touching payroll or tax matters, but worth restating here.

Returning to lighter matters: the annual budget planning kickoff meeting has been moved from November 18th to November 21st to accommodate scheduling conflicts with two regional VPs. Materials will be distributed the week prior, and each regional manager should come prepared with preliminary headcount projections for 2025.

Circling back to the Colorado notice one final time: I've asked our controller, Jason Whitfield, to draft a response letter along with the reconciliation documentation by November 14th so we have adequate time for internal review before the deadline. If any of you have visibility into payroll data for the Denver office during the transition period mentioned above, please reach out to Jason directly this week.

Lastly, a reminder that the holiday office closure schedule will be circulated by HR next week, and that expense reports for October are due by the 10th of this month as usual.

Please let me know if you have questions on any of the above, particularly regarding the Colorado withholding notice, as that item has the nearest deadline.

Priya
