INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: Department Heads, Facilities & Operations
From: Renata Osei, Director of Vendor Relations
Date: June 4, 2024
Re: Updated Janitorial Services Arrangement with Brightline Maintenance Group

This memo is intended to keep department heads informed of a recently finalized arrangement affecting building services across our downtown campus. As many of you are aware, our organization has occupied the Larkspur Tower location since 2011, and over that period we have worked with a number of vendors to maintain the cleanliness and operational readiness of our shared spaces. Before detailing the new arrangement, I want to take a moment to review some background on how our facilities program has evolved.

When the company first relocated to Larkspur Tower, janitorial and general maintenance services were handled internally by a small facilities staff of four employees. As headcount grew and the building expanded to occupy floors 6 through 14, it became clear that outsourcing these functions to a specialized vendor would allow our internal team to focus on higher-value facilities planning work, such as space utilization studies and energy efficiency initiatives. Since 2016, we have contracted with three different janitorial vendors, each selected through a competitive bidding process overseen by our procurement office.

I am writing to confirm that, effective July 1, 2024, we have entered into a signed service agreement with Brightline Maintenance Group, LLC, of Columbus, Ohio, to provide daily janitorial services, floor care, and window washing across floors 6 through 14 of Larkspur Tower. The agreement was executed on May 29, 2024, by our Chief Operating Officer, Marcus Feldt, and by Brightline's Managing Partner, Susan Whitcombe. Under the terms of the agreement, Brightline Maintenance Group will provide staffing of no fewer than six full-time equivalent workers on-site Monday through Friday between 6:00 PM and 11:00 PM, along with quarterly deep-cleaning services for all common areas, at a monthly rate of $18,400. The agreement further obligates Brightline to maintain general liability insurance of at least $2,000,000 and to carry workers' compensation coverage as required under Ohio law. Our organization, in turn, is obligated to provide secure after-hours building access, designated storage space for cleaning equipment on floors 6 and 10, and payment within 15 days of invoice receipt. The initial term of the agreement runs twelve months, with automatic renewal for successive one-year terms unless either party provides 60 days' written notice of termination.

As a standard reminder, all vendor agreements and related communications are considered confidential business information. Department heads should not share copies of vendor contracts, pricing schedules, or performance metrics outside of the organization without prior clearance from Legal and Procurement. This applies equally to draft materials, redlines, and any correspondence exchanged during negotiation.

For those interested in the broader context, our facilities program has also been exploring sustainability certifications for the Larkspur Tower campus, including LEED Operations and Maintenance recertification, which we expect to pursue in 2025. This is a separate initiative from the Brightline agreement and will be discussed in a future memo once more details are available from our sustainability consultant.

Department heads should direct any questions about access badges, storage arrangements, or scheduling conflicts with the new janitorial staff to Facilities Coordinator Priya Nandakumar. Questions regarding the commercial terms of the agreement itself, including billing disputes, should be routed to my office.

We appreciate your patience during the transition period as Brightline's staff becomes familiar with our building layout and existing protocols. A brief orientation session for building occupants will be scheduled for the week of July 8.

Regards,

Renata Osei
Director of Vendor Relations
