VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT NOTICE

Filing Reference: VSA-2024-0117
Parties: Meridian Logistics Group ("Client") andHarrowgate Facilities Management LLC ("Vendor")

This notice confirms that the Vendor Services Agreement between Meridian Logistics Group and Harrowgate Facilities Management LLC, executed on March 4, 2024, remains in full force and effect. The Agreement covers janitorial, grounds maintenance, and waste removal services for the Client's Newark and Trenton distribution centers.

Under the terms of the Agreement, Vendor shall provide services five days per week at each location, with monthly invoicing submitted no later than the 5th business day of the following month. The current term extends through March 3, 2026, with automatic renewal for successive one-year periods absent written notice of non-renewal delivered at least ninety days prior to expiration.

All obligations, service levels, and pricing schedules set forth in the original Agreement continue to apply without modification. Points of contact for administration of this Agreement are Denise Alvarado (Client) and Tomás Ibarra (Vendor).
