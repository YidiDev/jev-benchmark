INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: Regional Operations Managers
From: Sandra Ellis, Procurement Director
Date: February 9, 2024
Re: Status of Fleet Maintenance Services Agreement

This memo is to confirm that the Fleet Maintenance Services Agreement with Torrington Auto Care, originally executed on August 1, 2021, remains our current and active agreement for scheduled vehicle maintenance across all regional depots.

Under the terms of this agreement, Torrington Auto Care continues to provide oil changes, brake inspections, and tire rotation services for our delivery fleet on a rolling monthly schedule. Billing continues to be processed through the standard purchase order system, and their technicians remain authorized to perform work at any of our five service bays without additional sign-off.

Please continue routing all fleet maintenance requests through Torrington as usual. If your depot has been contacting an alternate provider, please redirect that work back to Torrington effective immediately, as they remain our sole contracted vendor for this service line.

Questions about scheduling or invoicing should be directed to my office.

Sandra Ellis
Procurement Director
