This letter confirms the terms of the Software Maintenance and Support Agreement between Bellcastle Systems, Inc. ("Provider") and Ridgeline Manufacturing ("Client"), effective March 15, 2023.

Under this Agreement, Provider shall furnish ongoing technical support, quarterly system updates, and a dedicated help desk line available Monday through Friday, 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Eastern Time, for the software platform licensed to Client under separate agreement. Client shall pay a monthly service fee of $4,200, invoiced on the first business day of each month.

Provider guarantees a response time of no more than four business hours for any priority-one incident reported through the designated support portal. This Agreement shall remain in effect for an initial term of twenty-four months, renewing automatically thereafter unless either party provides sixty days' written notice.

We look forward to a productive partnership and are committed to ensuring your systems run smoothly.
