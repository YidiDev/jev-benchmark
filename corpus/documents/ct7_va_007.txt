INTERNAL STATUS FILING

Date: August 8, 2024
Filed by: Merlin Team

This filing documents the status update submitted by the Merlin team for the reporting period of July 22 through August 5, 2024.

Progress on the archive digitization effort remains steady, with 1,240 of an estimated 1,800 records converted to date. Scanning throughput increased slightly following the replacement of a malfunctioning scanner unit on July 30. Quality checks on converted records show an error rate of under 1 percent, consistent with prior reporting periods.

A secondary task, reconciliation of legacy file labels against the new indexing system, is approximately 70 percent complete. Several discrepancies were found in older records dating prior to 2015; these have been flagged for manual review rather than automated correction to avoid introducing further inconsistencies.

Staffing remains unchanged from the previous period. One volunteer reviewer has offered to extend additional hours through the end of August to help close the gap on the reconciliation task.

No outstanding issues require immediate escalation. The next filing from the Merlin team is expected on or around August 19, 2024.
