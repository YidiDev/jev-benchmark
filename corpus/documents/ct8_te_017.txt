TO: All Department Heads, Finance and Accounting Staff
FROM: Marguerite Solano, Director of Corporate Tax Compliance
DATE: March 14, 2024
RE: Quarterly Tax Filing Notice and Withholding Statement Update

Colleagues,

Before addressing the matter at hand, I want to take a moment to remind everyone of the history behind this office. The Corporate Tax Compliance division of Halloway-Reinert Industries was established in 1987, originally as a two-person unit tasked with reconciling state sales tax remittances for our Ohio warehouses. Over the past three and a half decades, the division has grown to include eleven full-time analysts, a rotating cohort of two graduate interns each summer, and a dedicated liaison to our outside counsel at Prescott & Voss LLP. We take pride in the fact that Halloway-Reinert has never been assessed a late-filing penalty in excess of $500 in any calendar year since 1994, a streak we intend to protect.

As many of you know, the company has also recently updated its employee recognition program, and I encourage everyone to nominate colleagues who have gone above and beyond this quarter. Nomination forms are available on the intranet under "HR Resources," and the deadline for Q1 nominations is April 5.

Turning to the substance of this memo: this notice serves as formal internal confirmation that Halloway-Reinert Industries' federal quarterly withholding statement (Form 941) for the period ending December 31, 2023, has been filed with the Internal Revenue Service as of March 8, 2024. Total federal income tax withheld from employee wages for the quarter was $412,908.66, and the corresponding Social Security and Medicare withholding totaled $198,344.12. Combined with the employer's matching contribution, the total remittance to the IRS via EFTPS was $610,252.90, submitted on March 7, 2024, one day ahead of the statutory deadline.

In addition, the Ohio Department of Taxation has issued a preliminary assessment letter, dated February 26, 2024, indicating a proposed adjustment to our state withholding liability for the third quarter of 2023 in the amount of $14,776.30, attributable to a reclassification of seasonal warehouse staff from contract to employee status. Our tax counsel is currently reviewing the assessment and will respond within the thirty-day window permitted under Ohio Revised Code Section 5747.13. Department heads whose teams include seasonal or contract warehouse personnel should be prepared to provide supplemental payroll records upon request.

On an unrelated note, I want to remind staff that the company cafeteria will be closed for renovations from March 25 through April 1. Employees are encouraged to make alternate lunch arrangements during this period, and a list of nearby restaurants offering employee discounts has been posted near the elevator bank on the third floor.

Please also be reminded that this memo, along with any attachments, is intended solely for internal distribution among Halloway-Reinert Industries personnel and authorized advisors. It contains confidential financial and tax-related information and should not be forwarded outside the organization without prior written approval from the Legal Department. Unauthorized disclosure may result in disciplinary action and could expose the company to regulatory scrutiny. If you have received this memo in error, please notify the sender immediately and delete all copies.

As a final matter of general interest, Halloway-Reinert's origins trace back to a small machine shop founded by Edwin Halloway in Dayton, Ohio, in 1952. The company later merged with Reinert Fabrication Co. in 1979, forming the entity that exists today. We now operate four manufacturing facilities across the Midwest and employ approximately 1,150 people nationwide. Our mission remains rooted in the values Edwin Halloway espoused: precision, reliability, and community investment.

Please direct any questions regarding the withholding statement or the pending Ohio assessment to my office no later than March 22. Thank you for your continued diligence in this area.

Sincerely,
