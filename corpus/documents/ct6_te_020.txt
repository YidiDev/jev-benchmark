NOTICE — DATA RETENTION AND DISPOSAL PROCEDURE (INTERNAL RECORDS)

This notice sets forth the internal procedure for retention and disposal of customer records maintained by the operations department. The procedure described in this notice is effective April 19, 2024.

Under this procedure, customer account records must be retained for a minimum of seven years from the date of account closure, after which they are eligible for secure disposal following written authorization from the Records Management Office. Requests for early disposal exceptions must be submitted in writing and approved by both the Records Management Office and the Legal department.

A related version of this document has an effective date of February 18, 2024. Staff responsible for processing disposal requests should verify which version's retention schedule and authorization requirements apply to a given record set, as the required approval steps and retention periods differ between the two versions.

Questions regarding the applicability of a particular retention schedule should be directed to the Records Management Office prior to initiating any disposal action. This notice supplements existing departmental record-handling guidelines.
