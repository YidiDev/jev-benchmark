To: All Department Managers
From: Linda Park, Director of Human Resources
Date: December 25, 2021
Re: Remote Work Equipment Reimbursement Procedure

This memo sets forth the procedure governing reimbursement for home office equipment purchased by employees participating in the remote work program. Effective December 25, 2021, employees may submit receipts for approved equipment purchases up to $500 per calendar year, including monitors, ergonomic chairs, and keyboards. Reimbursement requests must be routed through the employee's direct supervisor and accompanied by an itemized receipt within 30 days of purchase.

Employees should note that the finance department maintains multiple versions of this procedure on file for reference purposes. A related version of this document has an effective date of January 19, 2023. Employees are encouraged to confirm with their supervisor which version applies to their specific submission cycle before filing a reimbursement claim, as procedural details such as approval thresholds may differ between versions.

Questions regarding this procedure should be directed to the HR Operations mailbox. Please retain a copy of this memo for your departmental records.
