INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: Executive Leadership Team
From: Bradford Aleman, Director of Tax Compliance
Date: November 21, 2024
Re: Federal Withholding Assessment Notice — Notice Number 88-2210-FW

This memo summarizes a federal withholding tax notice recently received from the Internal Revenue Service concerning Palisade Structural Engineering, PC.

The notice indicates that our reported federal income tax withholding for the second quarter of 2024 was understated. Per IRS records, total wages subject to withholding were $1,340,200.00, requiring withholding of $294,844.00. Our Form 941 filing reported only $281,900.00, resulting in an underpayment of $12,944.00.

The IRS has assessed a penalty of $647.20 for the shortfall, along with interest of $198.36 accrued from the original due date of July 31, 2024. Total amount owed, including penalty and interest, is $13,789.56.

Payment is due by December 15, 2024, to avoid further collection action. I recommend that our accounting team verify the wage figures against internal payroll records before we authorize payment or file an appeal.

Please let me know if you would like to schedule a call with our outside tax counsel to review this matter further before the deadline.
