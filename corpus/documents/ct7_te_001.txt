TO: Regional Operations Leadership
FROM: J. Alvarez, Team Lead
DATE: March 14, 2024
RE: Weekly Status Update — Distribution Node Performance

This memo provides the weekly status update filed by the Harrier team covering activity for the week of March 4–10.

Throughput at the eastern distribution node remained stable, averaging 94% of target capacity, a slight improvement over the prior week's 91%. Two minor equipment stoppages were logged on March 6 and March 8, both resolved within thirty minutes by on-site technicians. No safety incidents were reported during the period.

The Harrier team continues to monitor the newly installed sorting conveyor, which has now completed eleven days of operation without a recorded fault. Vendor follow-up on the replacement gearbox parts is scheduled for March 18.

Staffing remained at full complement for the week, with one planned absence covered by cross-trained personnel. Next week's focus will include a scheduled maintenance window on March 19 and continued tracking of throughput metrics ahead of the quarterly review.

Please direct any questions regarding this update to the undersigned.
