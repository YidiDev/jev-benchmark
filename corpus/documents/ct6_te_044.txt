Re: Employee Travel Reimbursement Agreement

Dear Ms. Fenwick,

This letter serves to formally issue the Employee Travel Reimbursement Agreement, effective September 9, 2023. This agreement establishes the terms under which employees may seek reimbursement for approved business travel expenses, including transportation, lodging, and per diem meal allowances, consistent with the guidelines maintained by the Finance Department.

Employees are required to submit expense reports within fourteen calendar days of travel completion, accompanied by original receipts for any single expense exceeding $75.00. Reimbursements will be processed through standard payroll channels within one billing cycle of approval.

For reference, a related version of this document has an effective date of March 4, 2025, and is on file with the Finance Department alongside this agreement. Employees or department heads seeking clarification on applicable terms for a particular travel period are encouraged to consult both versions directly or contact this office.

Please distribute this agreement to all department managers for acknowledgment by staff.

Sincerely,
