FILING RECORD: CONTRACT STATUS NOTICE
Matter: Equipment Maintenance Services Agreement, Dunmore Industrial Park

The Equipment Maintenance Services Agreement between Caldwell Industrial Holdings and Northbridge Technical Services, originally executed October 9, 2019, is recorded in this filing as no longer active.

This Agreement was superseded by the Amended and Restated Agreement dated January 25, 2022, which introduced revised response-time obligations for emergency repairs, updated parts replacement cost provisions, and added coverage for the newly installed conveyor systems at Building C.

From January 25, 2022 forward, the October 2019 agreement holds no binding effect between the parties. Personnel handling billing disputes, service escalation requests, or renewal negotiations must reference only the January 2022 Amended and Restated Agreement.

This notice is placed into the contract archive to prevent inadvertent reliance on the superseded terms. A copy of the current Amended and Restated Agreement is maintained by the Facilities Contracts office and available upon request to authorized personnel.
