SERVICE AGREEMENT NO. VA-2024-0119

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 4, 2024, by and between Brightline Facilities Group LLC ("Provider"), located at 4420 Corbett Ave, Suite 200, Denver, CO, and Halstead & Moore Retail Partners, Inc. ("Client"), located at 900 Larimer Plaza, Denver, CO.

1. Services. Provider shall furnish janitorial and grounds maintenance services to Client's six retail locations listed in Exhibit A, commencing April 1, 2024, and continuing through March 31, 2025.

2. Compensation. Client shall pay Provider a monthly fee of $8,750.00, due on the 5th of each month, payable via ACH transfer to the account specified in Exhibit B.

3. Term and Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement with sixty (60) days' written notice. Early termination fees of $2,500.00 apply if cancellation occurs before September 30, 2024.

4. Liability. Provider shall maintain general liability insurance of no less than $1,000,000 per occurrence throughout the term.
