REMOTE WORK POLICY — POLICY NO. HR-114

Effective Date: August 29, 2020

This document sets forth the guidelines governing remote work arrangements for employees of Halden Grove Logistics, including eligibility criteria, equipment provisioning, core availability hours, and expense reimbursement procedures applicable to staff working outside of a designated company office.

Section 1: Eligibility
Employees in good standing who have completed at least ninety (90) days of continuous employment may submit a remote work request to their direct supervisor using Form HR-114A.

Section 2: Equipment
The company will provide a laptop, monitor, and headset to approved remote employees. Employees are responsible for maintaining a secure and functional internet connection at their remote work location.

Section 3: Availability
Remote employees must be reachable via company communication channels between 9:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. local time on scheduled work days.

A related version of this document has an effective date of April 28, 2022.

Questions regarding this policy should be directed to the Human Resources department, Halden Grove Logistics.
