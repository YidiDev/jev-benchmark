TO: Finance and Legal Departments
FROM: Sandra Whitfield, Procurement Director
DATE: February 21, 2024
RE: Payroll Processing Services Agreement with Caldwell HR Partners

This memo confirms the current terms of our Payroll Processing Services Agreement with Caldwell HR Partners, executed on January 8, 2024. Under this agreement, Caldwell HR Partners manages biweekly payroll processing, tax filing, and benefits administration for all full-time and part-time employees across our three regional offices.

The agreement establishes a flat monthly service fee of $9,800, plus $6 per employee for benefits enrollment processing. Caldwell has assigned a dedicated payroll specialist, Ms. Renee Okafor, who serves as our primary point of contact for reconciliation issues and year-end tax reporting.

Finance should continue submitting hours and adjustment data by noon each Wednesday prior to the scheduled payroll run, in accordance with the agreement's processing timeline. Legal has reviewed the data-sharing provisions and confirmed compliance with applicable state privacy requirements.

Please direct any questions about payroll timing, fee structure, or reporting requirements to my office. I will provide quarterly updates on vendor performance as needed.
