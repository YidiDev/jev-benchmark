TO: Departmental Managers, Facilities & Procurement Group
FROM: Renata Ojukwu, Director of Vendor Relations
DATE: March 14, 2024
RE: Execution of Janitorial and Grounds Services Agreement with Meridian FacilityCare LLC

This memo is being circulated to confirm the finalization of our new service arrangement and to provide background context for staff who will be interfacing with the vendor's on-site personnel beginning next month.

By way of background, our company, Draymond Holt Industries, has occupied the Fairview Corporate Campus since 1998. Over the past twenty-six years, the campus has grown from a single 40,000-square-foot building to a four-building complex housing roughly 850 employees across engineering, logistics, and administrative functions. Facilities management has historically been handled through a rotating set of regional contractors, a practice that leadership determined was inefficient and inconsistent in quality. This history is provided simply so that new managers understand why a longer-term, centralized vendor relationship was deemed desirable.

Before turning to the specifics of the new arrangement, I want to remind all recipients of our standard confidentiality expectations. Any information regarding vendor pricing, internal facility layouts, security protocols, or personnel schedules shared in connection with this memo or its attachments is considered confidential company information. It should not be forwarded outside the organization, posted to shared drives accessible by non-employees, or discussed with representatives of competing facilities-service firms. Violations of this policy may be subject to review under the Employee Conduct Handbook, Section 7.

Turning now to the substance of this notice: effective April 1, 2024, Draymond Holt Industries has entered into a signed Master Services Agreement with Meridian FacilityCare LLC, a regional janitorial and grounds-maintenance provider headquartered in Council Bluffs, Iowa. Under the terms of the agreement, Meridian FacilityCare will provide daily janitorial services, weekly floor maintenance, and biweekly grounds and landscaping upkeep across all four Fairview Campus buildings. The agreement, executed by both parties on March 8, 2024, establishes a term of thirty-six months, with an initial contract value of $412,000 annually, payable in equal monthly installments of $34,333.33. Meridian FacilityCare is obligated to maintain minimum staffing levels of six full-time janitorial staff and two grounds crew members on-site during business hours, and to carry general liability insurance of no less than $2,000,000 per occurrence, naming Draymond Holt Industries as an additional insured party. Draymond Holt Industries, in turn, is obligated to provide secure storage space for equipment, timely payment per the schedule above, and thirty days' written notice prior to any facility closures affecting scheduled service.

For those unfamiliar with our broader vendor management philosophy, it may be useful to note that Draymond Holt Industries generally favors multi-year agreements over short-term contracts wherever feasible, as this approach has historically reduced onboarding costs and improved continuity of service quality. Our procurement team evaluated four competing bids before selecting Meridian FacilityCare, weighing factors including regional references, insurance coverage, and pricing structure.

As an appendix to this memo, attached please find a general overview of the Fairview Campus building directory, including elevator maintenance schedules and loading dock hours, which may be of incidental interest to managers coordinating with the new vendor's crews. This appendix is provided for convenience only and does not modify any terms of the agreement described above.

Please direct any questions regarding the agreement's implementation, including access badges for Meridian FacilityCare personnel, to the Facilities helpdesk. Questions regarding contract terms specifically should be routed to my office directly, as I served as the signatory on behalf of Draymond Holt Industries.

Thank you for your attention to this transition.

Renata Ojukwu
Director of Vendor Relations
Draymond Holt Industries
