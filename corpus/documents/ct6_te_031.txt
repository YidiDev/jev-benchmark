TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY — DOCUMENT REF. FIN-088

Effective Date: February 28, 2023

This policy governs all business travel arrangements, per diem allowances, and expense reporting requirements for employees of Cascade Ridge Manufacturing traveling on behalf of the company, whether domestic or international.

Booking Procedures
All travel bookings must be made through the approved corporate travel portal at least seven (7) business days in advance where feasible. Exceptions require written approval from a department head.

Per Diem Rates
Domestic per diem is set at $65 per day for meals and incidentals. International per diem varies by destination country and is listed in Appendix B of the full policy document.

Expense Submission
Employees must submit itemized receipts and a completed expense report within fourteen (14) calendar days of trip completion. Reports submitted after thirty (30) days require additional approval from the Finance Director.

A related version of this document has an effective date of March 27, 2023.

Employees with questions regarding this policy should contact the Finance department at Cascade Ridge Manufacturing headquarters.
