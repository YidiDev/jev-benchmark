NOTICE OF ACTIVE SERVICE AGREEMENT

Agreement Title: IT Support and Network Maintenance Agreement between Halden Manufacturing Co. and Vireo Systems Group
Effective Date: January 10, 2023
Contract Reference No.: HMC-VSG-2023-002

This notice confirms that Vireo Systems Group is currently engaged to provide ongoing information technology support services to Halden Manufacturing Co., including remote helpdesk assistance, network monitoring, server maintenance, and quarterly security audits for all facilities located at 2200 Industrial Parkway, Dayton, Ohio.

Under the terms of this Agreement, Vireo Systems Group maintains a guaranteed four-hour response time for critical outages and provides monthly performance reports to Halden Manufacturing Co.'s IT Director. Payment for services is rendered monthly at the flat rate specified in Schedule B, with additional project work billed hourly per Schedule C.

This Agreement remains in effect on a rolling annual basis, subject to the renewal terms outlined in Section 9. Either party may request a review of service levels during the annual renewal period. All notices regarding this Agreement should be directed to the designated contract administrators listed in Section 12.

Filed for administrative record purposes.
