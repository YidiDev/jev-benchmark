This letter serves as official notice of an assessment made against Thurgood Manufacturing Corp. regarding unpaid corporate income tax withholding obligations for the tax year ending December 31, 2023.

Our review of your filed Form OH-941 returns indicates a shortfall in remitted withholding taxes totaling $27,318.45. This figure reflects the difference between reported employee withholding of $198,450.00 and actual amounts deposited, which totaled $171,131.55.

Interest on the unpaid balance has accrued at a statutory rate of 6% annually, amounting to an additional $1,229.33 as of the date of this letter. The total amount now due is $28,547.78.

Please remit payment in full by July 31, 2024, to avoid further penalty accrual and potential referral for collections proceedings. Payment may be submitted by check to the address above, referencing Assessment ID GRT-77042.

Should you wish to dispute this assessment or request a payment plan, please contact our office within thirty days of receipt of this letter.
