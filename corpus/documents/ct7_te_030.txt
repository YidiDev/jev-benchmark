INTERNAL STATUS FILING

Reference Date: October 7, 2024
Filed by the Osprey team

Subject: Monthly Progress Update — Client Onboarding Workflow Review

This filing summarizes activity for the reporting window of September 1 through September 30, 2024. The Osprey team completed review of 48 client onboarding files, identifying 11 requiring corrected documentation and 37 approved without revision. Average processing time per file decreased from 5.4 days to 4.1 days compared to the prior month, attributed to the revised intake checklist introduced on September 3.

Two issues were escalated during the period: a recurring data mismatch in address verification (resolved September 19) and a temporary outage of the shared document repository lasting approximately six hours on September 24, which delayed twelve pending reviews. Both matters have since been closed.

Staffing note: team member Rosalind Hayes transitioned to part-time status effective September 16; a replacement analyst is expected to join by October 21.

Next filing is anticipated on or before November 7, 2024, covering the October reporting period. No further action is requested from recipients at this time; this filing is submitted for record-keeping and informational purposes only.
