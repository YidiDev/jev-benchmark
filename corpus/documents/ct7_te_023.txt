TO: Regional Operations Leads
FROM: Alan Whitfield
DATE: September 12
RE: Weekly Progress Summary

This memo is submitted on behalf of the Merlin team to provide an update on activities from the past week.

Warehouse reorganization at the north facility is roughly 70% complete, with the remaining shelving units scheduled to be installed by September 19. Inventory counts conducted mid-week showed a discrepancy of 34 units in the electronics category, which has been escalated to the audit group for reconciliation.

Staff training on the new handheld scanning devices concluded Wednesday, with 22 of 25 employees completing certification. The three remaining staff will complete makeup sessions early next week.

On the customer-facing side, order fulfillment times improved to an average of 1.8 days, down from 2.4 days the prior period, largely due to the reorganized pick paths.

One outstanding issue: the loading dock door sensor continues to malfunction intermittently. A repair technician has been scheduled for September 15.

We anticipate the reorganization project reaching full completion by the end of next week and will report final metrics at that time.

Regards,
Alan Whitfield
