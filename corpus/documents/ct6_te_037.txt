DATE: February 20, 2024
RE: Procurement Approval Thresholds Policy

This memo introduces the updated procurement approval thresholds policy, which carries an effective date of February 20, 2024. The policy sets forth the dollar-value thresholds requiring departmental director sign-off, vice-presidential approval, and full procurement committee review for purchase orders issued by Bramwell Industrial Supply Co.

Key threshold levels are unchanged in structure from prior guidance but have been reformatted for clarity and cross-referenced with the vendor onboarding checklist maintained by Accounts Payable. A related version of this document has an effective date of August 18, 2025, and department heads should be aware that both versions may appear in shared procurement folders during the transition period.

Please review the attached threshold table with your team leads and route any purchase requisitions above $10,000 through the standard approval workflow described in Section 4. Direct questions to my office or to the Procurement Coordinator, Ms. Feyre Adjei, who can be reached at extension 4471.

Reginald Osei
Chief Procurement Officer
Bramwell Industrial Supply Co.
