NOTICE OF SUPERSEDED VENDOR AGREEMENT

Reference: Equipment Leasing Agreement between Draystone Manufacturing Inc. and Falcon Industrial Equipment Corp.
Original Execution Date: March 1, 2020
Filing Date: September 22, 2023

This notice is filed to update internal contract records regarding the above-referenced Equipment Leasing Agreement, which governed the lease of forklifts, pallet jacks, and loading dock equipment at the Draystone Manufacturing facility located at 1150 Foundry Road.

This Agreement was superseded by the Amended and Restated Agreement dated August 14, 2023, which revised the monthly lease payment structure from a flat rate to a tiered usage-based model and extended the lease term by an additional 24 months. The amended agreement also incorporates updated liability and insurance provisions not present in the original document.

All departments referencing lease terms, payment schedules, or equipment coverage for this vendor relationship should consult the August 14, 2023 amended agreement exclusively. The original March 2020 agreement is retained for historical record purposes only and no longer governs the parties' obligations.

Filed by: Contracts Administration Office
Document Control Number: CA-2023-1148

END OF NOTICE
