Dear Committee Members,

Please accept this letter as the quarterly status update filed by the Falcon team for the period ending September 30, 2024.

Over the past quarter, the team completed migration of the legacy inventory records to the new tracking system, closing out 214 of 230 outstanding tickets. The remaining sixteen items are scheduled for resolution by October 18, pending vendor confirmation on two hardware replacements. Staffing remained stable, with one new analyst, Priya Chandrasekar, onboarded on September 9.

We also conducted a satisfaction survey among internal stakeholders, receiving a 91% approval rating for turnaround times, a four-point improvement over last quarter. A detailed breakdown is available upon request.

Submitted on behalf of Team Falcon, we thank the committee for its continued support and look forward to discussing these results at the next quarterly review.

Sincerely,
