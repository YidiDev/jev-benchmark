Before I get into the specifics of the outstanding balance, I wanted to take a moment to share some background on Brightline Fabrication Co., since we have a number of new staff in your billing department who may not be familiar with our company history and how our working relationship with Hollis Manufacturing Group began.

Brightline Fabrication Co. was founded in 1987 by machinists Arthur and Eileen Brightline in a converted grain warehouse outside Dayton, Ohio. What began as a two-person shop producing custom steel brackets for the regional agricultural industry has grown into a 340-employee operation spanning three facilities in Ohio and Indiana. We take considerable pride in our craftsmanship heritage, and every new hire at Brightline still tours our original Dayton shop floor as part of onboarding, where several of Arthur's original hand tools remain on display in a small glass case near the break room.

Our partnership with Hollis Manufacturing Group dates back to 2016, when we were first subcontracted to fabricate custom ventilation housings for the Copper Ridge facility expansion. Since then, we have completed eleven separate project phases together, and we consider Hollis one of our most valued long-term clients.
