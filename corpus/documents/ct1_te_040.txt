This letter serves to formalize the Security Services Agreement between Ironclad Security Solutions ("Provider") and Bellweather Hotels Group ("Client"), signed by both parties on May 17, 2024.

Provider agrees to furnish uniformed security personnel for overnight coverage at the Bellweather downtown property, seven nights per week, for a term of one year commencing June 1, 2024. Client agrees to pay Provider a monthly service fee of $14,500, invoiced on the last business day of each month with payment due within 15 days.

Provider shall ensure all assigned personnel hold current state security licenses and shall carry general liability insurance of no less than $2,000,000. Client shall provide adequate facilities for staff check-in and equipment storage on-site. This agreement may be terminated by either party with 30 days' written notice, or immediately in the event of a material breach.

We appreciate the opportunity to partner with Bellweather Hotels Group and look forward to a successful engagement.
