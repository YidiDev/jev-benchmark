To: Department Heads and Team Leads
From: Rachel Simmons, Chief Compliance Officer
Date: August 14, 2022
Re: Conflict of Interest Disclosure Procedure

This memo restates the internal procedure governing annual conflict of interest disclosures required of all employees at the manager level and above. The procedure set out in this memo is effective August 14, 2022. Employees subject to this requirement must complete the disclosure form distributed by the Compliance Office and return it within 15 business days of receipt. Disclosures must be updated within 30 days of any material change in circumstances, including new outside employment, board positions, or family relationships involving vendors.

For internal record purposes, a related version of this document has an effective date of January 17, 2022. Employees or managers reviewing archived compliance files should note that the disclosure timelines and reporting thresholds described in each version are not identical, and should confirm which version governed a given disclosure cycle before drawing conclusions from historical records.

Direct any questions regarding current disclosure obligations to the Compliance Office at extension 4471.
