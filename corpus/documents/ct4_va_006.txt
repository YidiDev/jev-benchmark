NOTICE OF RECORD STATUS – VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

Agreement Title: Facilities Maintenance Services Agreement between Brightline Property Group LLC and Corestone Janitorial Services Inc.
Original Effective Date: March 4, 2019
Contract Reference No.: BPG-CJS-2019-014

This document confirms the terms under which Corestone Janitorial Services Inc. agreed to provide daily custodial and grounds maintenance services to the properties owned and managed by Brightline Property Group LLC, located at 4410 Westgate Commons, Units A through F. Services under this Agreement included nightly cleaning, waste removal, seasonal landscaping, and snow removal during the winter months, billed monthly at the rate specified in Exhibit A.

The Agreement further outlined insurance requirements, indemnification provisions, and a 60-day termination-for-convenience clause available to either party.

This Agreement was superseded by the Amended and Restated Facilities Maintenance Services Agreement dated September 17, 2022, which incorporates revised pricing schedules and expanded service locations. All parties should refer exclusively to the September 17, 2022 Amended and Restated Agreement for current terms, obligations, and points of contact.

Filed for record retention purposes only.
