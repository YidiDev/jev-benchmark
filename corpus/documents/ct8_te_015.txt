We hope this letter finds you and the Northgate Manufacturing team well as you close out the fiscal year. Cascadia Revenue Partners was established in 1995 by a small group of former state revenue auditors who wanted to bring a more collaborative, client-centered approach to corporate tax compliance. Over the past thirty years, our firm has expanded to three regional offices in Seattle, Spokane, and Boise, and we now employ over sixty tax professionals specializing in manufacturing, agriculture, and logistics sector clients.

Our firm maintains a strict confidentiality policy consistent with industry standards. Any information contained in this letter, including attachments, is intended solely for the addressee and authorized personnel of Northgate Manufacturing Co. Unauthorized disclosure, copying, or distribution of this correspondence is prohibited. If you believe you have received this letter in error, please notify our office immediately and destroy all copies.

This letter constitutes formal notification of a state tax assessment concerning Northgate Manufacturing Co.'s business and occupation tax filings for the period covering July 1 through December 31, 2024. Our review of the quarterly returns submitted to the Washington State Department of Revenue indicates an underpayment in the amount of $27,845.60, attributable to an incorrect classification of wholesale distribution revenue as retail sales during the third quarter. This misclassification affected the applicable tax rate applied to approximately $1.9 million in gross receipts.

Cascadia Revenue Partners takes pride in being an active member of the Puget Sound business community. Each fall, our firm sponsors a scholarship fund for accounting students at three regional community colleges, and our staff regularly volunteer with local nonprofit organizations focused on financial literacy education. We believe that strong communities and strong businesses go hand in hand, and we are proud to have contributed over $180,000 in scholarship funding since the program's inception in 2010.

Returning to the assessment described above, we recommend that Northgate Manufacturing file a corrected excise tax return with the Department of Revenue no later than February 28, 2025. Failure to remit the corrected amount by this date will result in a penalty of five percent of the unpaid tax, plus statutory interest accruing at a rate determined annually by the Department. As of the date of this letter, estimated interest on the underpayment totals $563.40, though this figure will increase if payment is delayed beyond the stated deadline.

Enclosed with this letter you will find a detailed worksheet outlining the revised revenue classifications and corresponding tax calculations for your internal review (see Attachment B). We also recommend that your accounting staff review invoicing procedures for wholesale accounts going forward to prevent similar misclassifications in future filing periods.
