TO: Regional Store Managers
FROM: Oliver Brandt, Supply Chain Coordinator
DATE: May 30, 2023
RE: Refrigeration Maintenance Agreement with ColdChain Services Inc.

This memo outlines the terms of our current Refrigeration Maintenance Agreement with ColdChain Services Inc., executed on April 3, 2023. The agreement covers preventive maintenance, emergency repair calls, and parts replacement for all commercial refrigeration units across our twelve retail locations.

ColdChain guarantees a four-hour response time for emergency service calls and conducts scheduled maintenance visits every six weeks. The monthly service fee is $3,150 per location, with parts and labor for major repairs billed separately according to the attached rate schedule.

Store managers should contact ColdChain's dispatch line directly at (555) 019-2244 for any refrigeration issues, rather than routing calls through corporate maintenance. Please log all service visits in the facilities tracking system so we can monitor compliance with the response time guarantees.

Questions regarding coverage, billing, or the rate schedule should be directed to my office. I will circulate updated contact information if ColdChain makes any staffing changes to their dispatch team.
