To: Mr. Owen Ferraro, Vice President of Client Services

Dear Mr. Ferraro,

This letter serves as our monthly status report for October activity. Submitted on behalf of Team Kestrel, it outlines progress made on the client reconciliation backlog initiative launched earlier this quarter.

During October, we cleared 342 of the 410 outstanding reconciliation cases identified in September, bringing our completion rate to just over 83 percent. Three cases required escalation to the compliance office due to discrepancies exceeding our standard tolerance threshold, and those remain under review. We also implemented a revised checklist for intake staff, which has already reduced re-opened cases by roughly a third.

For November, our goal is to close the remaining backlog entirely and shift focus toward preventive measures, including a proposed quarterly audit cadence. We welcome any guidance on prioritization should new cases arrive before the current backlog is resolved.

Please let us know if you require additional detail on any of the figures cited above.

Respectfully,
