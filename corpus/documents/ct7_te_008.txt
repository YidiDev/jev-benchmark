STATUS UPDATE — FILING RECORD

Date Filed: September 12

Submitted on behalf of Team Harrier.

This filing documents current progress on the ongoing records-consolidation initiative under review by the Harrier team.

As of this date, 68% of the legacy case files have been transferred to the new archival format, up from 51% at the last reporting interval on August 15. The remaining files are concentrated in the 2018–2019 archive batch, which has required additional manual indexing due to inconsistent original formatting.

Two members of the Harrier team, S. Almeida and R. Duquette, have been temporarily reassigned from other duties to accelerate the indexing effort. This reassignment is expected to continue through the end of September.

No significant obstacles have been identified this period. A minor delay occurred in early September when the scanning equipment on the third floor required servicing, resulting in approximately four days of reduced throughput. The equipment has since been repaired and is operating normally.

The Harrier team anticipates completing the consolidation project by October 30, pending no further equipment issues or staffing changes.

Filed by: L. Marchetti, on behalf of the Harrier team.
