This letter documents the terms of the Vehicle Maintenance Services Agreement entered into between Kestrel Fleet Maintenance, LLC ("Contractor") and Persimmon Logistics ("Client"), effective November 20, 2020, for the scheduled servicing and emergency repair of Client's delivery fleet.

Contractor agreed to provide monthly inspections, oil changes, and brake servicing for up to forty vehicles at a rate of $310 per vehicle per month, with emergency roadside repair available within a 50-mile radius of the Toledo depot.

Please be advised that this Agreement was superseded by the Amended and Restated Agreement dated August 14, 2022, which incorporated additional coverage for refrigerated trailer units and revised the monthly rate structure. This original Agreement no longer governs the current relationship between the parties and is provided here for archival purposes only.
