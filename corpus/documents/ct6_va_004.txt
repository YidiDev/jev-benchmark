POLICY NOTICE: REMOTE EQUIPMENT REIMBURSEMENT PROCEDURE

Effective Date: October 5, 2024

This notice establishes the procedure by which employees may submit claims for reimbursement of home office equipment purchases, including monitors, ergonomic chairs, and docking stations, up to a per-employee annual limit of $450.

To submit a claim, employees must complete Form RE-12 and attach an itemized receipt dated within the current reimbursement cycle. Claims must be routed through the employee's direct manager for approval prior to submission to Accounts Payable. Reimbursements will generally be processed within fifteen business days of approval.

Equipment purchased for shared or team use, rather than individual use, is not eligible under this procedure and should instead be routed through standard departmental purchasing channels.

A related version of this document has an effective date of November 7, 2023. Employees with questions regarding which version applies to a specific purchase or claim period should contact Human Resources at extension 4471 or via the internal helpdesk portal for clarification prior to submitting Form RE-12.

This notice is maintained by the Human Resources Operations group and is reviewed on a periodic basis.
