PROJECT EPSILON — Q3 STATUS UPDATE
Internal Report | Distribution: Program Office, Engineering Leads

Development work on the Epsilon data-pipeline module continued through the quarter, with the integration testing phase now 80% complete. The team completed the schema migration ahead of schedule and resolved two outstanding defects flagged during the July code review.
