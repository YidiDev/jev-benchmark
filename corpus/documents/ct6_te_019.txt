TO: All Regional Branch Managers
FROM: Ottilie Vance, Director of Compliance
DATE: August 10, 2023
RE: Vendor Code of Conduct — Policy Update

This memo accompanies the vendor code of conduct document now circulated to all regional offices. The current version carries an effective date of August 10, 2023, and sets forth expectations for third-party vendors and contractors engaged by Sablewood Financial Partners, including provisions on anti-bribery compliance, data handling standards, and conflict-of-interest disclosures.

Please note that a related version of this document has an effective date of July 10, 2022. Both versions address substantially the same subject matter, and branch managers should confirm with their assigned compliance liaison which copy is applicable to any vendor contract currently under negotiation.

Branch managers are asked to distribute the accompanying acknowledgment form to all active vendors within thirty (30) days and to return signed copies to the Compliance office at headquarters. Any questions regarding specific provisions can be directed to my office or to the regional compliance liaison assigned to your branch.

Please confirm receipt of this memo by reply email.

Ottilie Vance
Director of Compliance
Sablewood Financial Partners
