NOTICE OF INTERNAL POLICY — REMOTE WORK ARRANGEMENTS

Policy Reference: HR-RW-114
Effective Date: February 24, 2026

This notice sets forth the Remote Work Arrangements policy applicable to all salaried employees of Marchbanks Logistics Group across its Ohio and Kentucky facilities. The policy governs eligibility criteria for remote work requests, equipment provisioning standards, core availability hours, and the procedure for supervisor approval and periodic review of remote work agreements.

Employees seeking remote work status must submit Form HR-224 to their direct supervisor at least fifteen business days prior to the requested start date. Supervisors are required to document approval or denial within five business days of receipt, with a copy forwarded to Human Resources for the employee's personnel file.

A related version of this document has an effective date of April 5, 2025. Employees and managers referencing this policy for the purposes of scheduling, equipment requests, or dispute resolution should confirm which effective date applies to their circumstances by checking the version number printed in the document footer.

Questions regarding this policy should be directed to the Human Resources Business Partner assigned to the employee's department.
