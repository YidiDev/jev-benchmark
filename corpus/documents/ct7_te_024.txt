MEMORANDUM

To: Interdepartmental Coordination Desk
From: Team Condor
Date: March 4, 2025
Re: Monthly Update — Vendor Compliance Review

This memo is filed by the Condor team to report on activities completed during February 2025 related to the vendor compliance review.

Of the 62 vendor files scheduled for review this cycle, 55 were completed and closed as compliant. Five files were flagged for missing insurance documentation and returned to the respective vendors for correction; two remain outstanding as of this writing, with follow-up communication sent on February 26. One file was escalated to legal review due to a discrepancy in contract dates and remains pending.

The team also updated the internal compliance checklist to include a new field for data-handling certifications, effective February 17, following feedback gathered during January's review cycle.

No staffing changes occurred this period. Team member Oscar Delacroix will be on approved leave from March 10 through March 21; coverage has been arranged internally.

Submitted on behalf of Team Condor, the next monthly update is expected on or around April 4, 2025.
