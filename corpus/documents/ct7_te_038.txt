INTERNAL STATUS FILING

Date: April 11, 2024
Subject: Bi-Weekly Progress Notice — Facility Retrofit Initiative

Filed by the Raven team, this notice summarizes progress made between March 28 and April 11, 2024, on the ongoing HVAC retrofit project at the Northside Distribution Center.

During this period, the team completed removal of the legacy ductwork on the east wing and installed 60% of the new insulated piping. Inspection by the facilities safety officer took place on April 8 and produced no corrective actions. Material deliveries scheduled for April 15 will allow work to proceed on the west wing ahead of the original projected start date of April 22.

One delay was noted: the electrical subcontractor requested a two-day extension due to a permitting backlog with the city office. This is not expected to affect the overall project completion date of June 30.

Budget tracking remains within approved limits, with 41% of allocated funds expended to date against 45% of scheduled work completed.

The next progress notice will be filed on or before April 25, 2024.

Submitted for the record by Raven team administrative staff.
