MEMORANDUM

To: All Staff
From: Marcus Delaney, HR Operations Manager
Date: April 19, 2025
Re: Paid Time Off Accrual and Carryover Procedure

This memo distributes the Paid Time Off Accrual and Carryover Procedure, effective April 19, 2025. The procedure describes the monthly PTO accrual rate for full-time and part-time employees, the process for requesting carryover of unused hours into the following calendar year, and the documentation required for PTO donation to colleagues under the company's leave-sharing program.

Please be aware that a related version of this document has an effective date of March 20, 2025. Both versions are currently referenced in different onboarding checklists depending on hire date, so employees who joined earlier this year should confirm with their HR representative which version applies to their accrual calculations.

Managers should ensure that team members reviewing PTO balances in the payroll portal cross-check the accrual table in the attached procedure against their individual employment start dates.

Direct all inquiries to the HR Operations helpdesk or your assigned HR business partner.

Marcus Delaney
HR Operations Manager
