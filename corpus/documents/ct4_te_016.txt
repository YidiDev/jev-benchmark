To: Facilities and Procurement Staff
From: Robert Guillen, Operations Manager
Date: April 11, 2024
Re: Janitorial Services Agreement with Clearview Building Solutions

This memo confirms the terms of our current agreement with Clearview Building Solutions for janitorial and facility maintenance services at the downtown office location.

Under the agreement, Clearview provides nightly cleaning services Monday through Friday, biweekly carpet maintenance, and monthly deep-cleaning of common areas. The monthly service fee is $4,850, invoiced on the first business day of each month. The agreement designates Clearview's on-site supervisor, Marcus Feld, as the primary point of contact for scheduling and service issues.

Staff should continue to submit any facility cleanliness concerns through the standard maintenance ticketing system so they can be routed to Clearview per the terms of the agreement. Please do not contact Clearview directly for scheduling changes, as this must go through Procurement to remain consistent with the agreed service protocol.

Questions about the scope of services can be directed to my office.

Robert Guillen
Operations Manager
