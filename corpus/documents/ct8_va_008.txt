ADMINISTRATIVE FILING NOTICE
Reference No. AF-2024-0317

This filing is submitted for the internal records of Caldwell & Pruitt Facilities Management and is being circulated to relevant departmental stakeholders for informational purposes. Caldwell & Pruitt was established in 1994 as a regional janitorial and grounds maintenance provider and has since grown to service over 200 commercial properties across three states. The company's mission statement, adopted by the board of directors in 2003 and reaffirmed most recently in 2021, emphasizes reliability, environmental stewardship, and responsiveness to client needs across all lines of service.

Historically, Caldwell & Pruitt has maintained a diversified vendor network to support its facilities operations, including relationships with landscaping subcontractors, HVAC technicians, and pest control specialists. The company periodically reviews and formalizes these vendor relationships through written agreements to ensure clarity of obligations and to protect both parties in the event of disputes. The following notice documents one such formalization.

On March 4, 2024, Caldwell & Pruitt Facilities Management ("Client") and Thornbury Pest Solutions LLC ("Contractor") executed a Service Agreement governing the provision of quarterly pest control treatments at twelve designated properties located within Hennepin and Ramsey Counties, Minnesota. Under the terms of the agreement, Contractor agrees to perform inspection and treatment services no less than four times per calendar year at each designated property, with the first treatment cycle beginning no later than April 15, 2024. Client agrees to pay Contractor a flat quarterly fee of $3,600.00 per property cluster, payable within twenty-one (21) days of invoice receipt, for a total estimated annual expenditure of $14,400.00 across the agreement term. The agreement further specifies that Contractor shall carry general liability insurance of no less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and shall provide Client with proof of insurance renewal annually. The agreement was signed by MargaretYu, Director of Facilities Operations, on behalf of Caldwell & Pruitt, and by Dennis Okafor, Managing Partner, on behalf of Thornbury Pest Solutions LLC, and is effective through March 3, 2026, unless terminated earlier pursuant to Section 9 (Termination for Convenience), which permits either party to end the agreement with sixty (60) days' written notice.

As a matter of standing policy, all filings of this nature are accompanied by a confidentiality reminder. The contents of this notice, including any financial terms, vendor names, or property identifiers referenced herein, are intended for internal distribution only and should not be shared outside of Caldwell & Pruitt Facilities Management without prior written authorization from the Director of Facilities Operations. Employees who receive this notice in error, or who are uncertain whether they are authorized recipients, should contact the Records Compliance Office at extension 4471 before forwarding or discussing its contents.

For general background, Caldwell & Pruitt's Records Compliance Office also maintains an archive of all executed vendor agreements dating back to 2010, organized by service category and renewal date. This archive is reviewed annually by the Legal and Procurement departments to ensure that expired or soon-to-expire agreements are flagged for renewal discussions well in advance of their termination dates. The current review cycle is scheduled to begin in September 2024 and will include an audit of insurance certificates on file for all active pest control, landscaping, and janitorial subcontractors.

Additionally, the agreement described above includes a standard indemnification clause (Section 11) requiring Contractor to hold Client harmless from claims arising out of Contractor's negligent performance of services, as well as a dispute resolution clause (Section 14) specifying binding arbitration in Ramsey County, Minnesota, as the preferred mechanism for resolving disagreements that cannot be settled informally between the parties.

This notice has been filed with the Records Compliance Office and copied to the Facilities Operations department for tracking purposes. No further action is required from recipients unless otherwise instructed by the Director of Facilities Operations.
