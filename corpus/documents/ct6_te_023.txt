Re: Data Retention and Records Disposal Policy

Dear Managers,

This letter transmits the Data Retention and Records Disposal Policy, effective April 4, 2027. This policy applies to all regional offices and governs the retention schedules for client files, internal correspondence, and financial records, as well as the approved methods for secure disposal once retention periods have elapsed.

Under this policy, client engagement files must be retained for seven years following the closure of an engagement, after which they are eligible for secure shredding or certified digital deletion, subject to sign-off from the Compliance Office. Financial records subject to regulatory requirements follow the retention schedule maintained separately by the Controller's Office.

A related version of this document has an effective date of May 13, 2025. Office managers should confirm with the Compliance Office which version applies to records currently under their custody.

Please ensure this policy is reviewed with all staff responsible for records management in your office.

Sincerely,
