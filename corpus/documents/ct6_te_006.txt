Re: Workplace Safety Reporting and Incident Response Policy

Dear Colleagues,

This letter formally issues the Workplace Safety Reporting and Incident Response Policy, effective April 22, 2022. This policy establishes the requirements for reporting workplace injuries, near-miss events, and equipment malfunctions across all Castlewood Manufacturing facilities.

Under this policy, any injury, regardless of severity, must be reported to the on-site safety officer within one hour of occurrence, and a written incident report must be filed within 24 hours through the internal safety portal. Near-miss events involving heavy machinery must additionally be escalated to the Regional Safety Director for review.

A related version of this document has an effective date of April 4, 2022. Supervisors should confirm with the Regional Safety Director which version applies to their facility before conducting shift briefings referencing this policy.

Please post this notice on all shift-floor bulletin boards and incorporate its requirements into upcoming safety briefings.

Sincerely,

Walter Bienemann
Director of Environmental Health and Safety
