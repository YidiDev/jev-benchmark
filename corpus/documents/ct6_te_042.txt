NOTICE OF INTERNAL POLICY — EMPLOYEE TRAVEL AND REIMBURSEMENT PROCEDURE

Policy Reference: FIN-TR-208
Effective Date: March 29, 2025

This notice establishes the procedure for booking, documenting, and reimbursing employee business travel expenses. Under this policy, all domestic travel must be booked through the approved corporate travel portal, and airfare exceeding $600 requires prior manager approval. Employees traveling internationally must submit a travel authorization form no later than ten business days before departure.

Meal reimbursements are capped at $65 per day for domestic travel and $85 per day for international travel, with itemized receipts required for any single expense over $25. Mileage for personal vehicle use is reimbursed at the standard rate published quarterly by the Finance Department.

A related version of this document has an effective date of October 5, 2025. Employees submitting reimbursement claims should identify which version applies to the travel dates in question, as reimbursement caps and documentation requirements may be referenced differently depending on the applicable version.

Direct questions regarding this procedure to the Finance Department travel desk.
