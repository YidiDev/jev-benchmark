MEMORANDUM

To: Regional Operations Leadership
From: Team Merlin
Date: November 12, 2024
Re: Weekly Status Update — Facilities Transition Project

This memo is filed by the Merlin team to provide the routine weekly update on the ongoing facilities transition project.

As of this week, relocation of the third-floor archive materials to the offsite storage facility is 78% complete, with the remaining boxes expected to be transferred by November 22. The delay from the originally projected November 15 completion date is attributed to a shortage of pallet lifts, which has since been resolved through a rental agreement with a local supplier.

Two minor incidents were logged this week: a mislabeled shipment of records (corrected the same day) and a brief elevator outage that paused transport activity for roughly ninety minutes on November 8. Neither incident affected the overall timeline.

Personnel note: contractor Dennis Okafor joined the team on November 4 to assist with cataloging efforts, bringing the on-site crew to six members.

Submitted on behalf of Team Merlin, this update will be followed by the next scheduled report on November 19.

Regards,
Team Merlin
