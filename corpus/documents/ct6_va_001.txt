To: Regional Branch Managers
From: Denise Okafor, Operations Compliance
Date: August 9, 2023
Re: Cash Handling and Deposit Procedure

This memo issues the Cash Handling and Deposit Procedure, effective August 9, 2023, for all branch locations processing daily cash receipts.

Under this procedure, all cash drawers must be reconciled and counted by two employees at the close of each business day, with discrepancies over $10 reported immediately to the branch manager. Deposits must be prepared using the dual-control method and transported to the bank within one business day using an approved courier service.

Branch managers should note that a related version of this document has an effective date of July 26, 2023. Staff transferring between branches or reviewing archived training materials should confirm with their manager which version applies to their current location's procedures.

All reconciliation logs and deposit slips must be retained on-site for ninety days and made available for audit upon request. Any discrepancies exceeding $50 should be escalated directly to Regional Operations rather than resolved at the branch level.

Please review this memo with your teller staff during the next scheduled shift meeting and confirm receipt with the Operations Compliance office.
