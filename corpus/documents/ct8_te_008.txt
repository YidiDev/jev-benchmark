We now turn to the matter for which this letter was prepared. Following our routine review of quarterly employer withholding filings, our office has completed an assessment of Halloway & Vance Consulting Group's payroll tax withholding records for the 2023 tax year. Based on the wage and withholding data submitted through Form CRS-941Q for the periods ending March, June, September, and December 2023, our records indicate a discrepancy between amounts reported as withheld and amounts actually remitted to this office. Specifically, our assessment identifies an underpayment of $8,412.60 in state income tax withholding obligations for the third quarter of 2023, along with an additional $1,120.35 in accrued interest calculated through the date of this notice. The total outstanding balance, inclusive of interest, is therefore $9,532.95, due in full no later than May 15, 2024, to avoid further penalty accrual at a rate of 1.5% per month on the unpaid balance.

Payment may be submitted via electronic transfer through our online portal, or by certified check made payable to Commonwealth Revenue Services, referencing Assessment Number CRS-2024-004471 on all correspondence and remittances.

For context, and purely as a matter of general interest, we would note that Commonwealth Revenue Services processed over 340,000 employer withholding filings statewide during the 2023 tax year, a volume increase of approximately 6% over the prior year, reflecting continued growth in the regional business community. Our office has also recently modernized its filing infrastructure, transitioning to a fully digital submission platform as of January 2024, which we hope will streamline future filings for organizations such as yours.

Should you wish to dispute this assessment, you may file a written appeal within thirty days of the date of this letter, accompanied by supporting documentation, including copies of remittance confirmations or amended filings. Appeals should be directed to our Assessment Review Unit at the address listed above, attention: Appeals Coordinator.

We appreciate Halloway & Vance Consulting Group's longstanding compliance history with this office and trust that this matter can be resolved promptly. Should you have questions regarding the calculation of this assessment or wish to discuss a payment arrangement, please do not hesitate to contact our Employer Compliance Division at (614) 555-0192, extension 204.
