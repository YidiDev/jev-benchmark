INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: All Department Managers
From: Sylvia Renquist, Director of Human Resources
Date: March 21, 2023
Re: Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual and Carryover Policy

This memo distributes the Paid Time Off Accrual and Carryover Policy applicable to all full-time employees of Coralwood Financial Services, effective March 21, 2023. The policy details accrual rates by tenure band, the maximum carryover permitted into the following calendar year, and the procedure for requesting an exception to the carryover cap through the HR portal.

Managers should note that a related version of this document has an effective date of March 6, 2023. Both versions contain the same accrual tables, though the sections addressing exception requests differ slightly in format. Managers reviewing employee PTO balances during the upcoming quarterly audit should confirm which version was in effect at the time a given accrual or exception was recorded.

Please distribute this memo to your direct reports and retain a copy for your departmental records. Any questions regarding accrual calculations should be routed to HR Operations rather than to individual payroll contacts.

Thank you for your attention to this matter.
