CONTRACT STATUS NOTICE

Notice No.: CS-2021-114
Subject: Equipment Maintenance Agreement — Falcon Industrial Services and Ridgeline Manufacturing Corp.

This notice pertains to the Equipment Maintenance Agreement entered into between Falcon Industrial Services ("Contractor") and Ridgeline Manufacturing Corp. ("Client") on May 3, 2021, covering preventive maintenance and emergency repair services for the Client's press-line machinery at its Youngstown facility.

Under the original terms, Contractor was required to perform quarterly inspections and to guarantee emergency response within eight hours of a service request. Annual fees were fixed at $84,000, payable in equal quarterly installments.

This notice serves to confirm that the above-referenced Agreement is no longer in effect as originally executed. This Agreement was superseded by the Amended and Restated Agreement dated November 15, 2022, which expanded coverage to include the Client's newly installed stamping equipment and reduced the emergency response window to four hours. Effective upon that date, all rights and obligations of the parties are governed exclusively by the Amended and Restated Agreement.

Filed by: Legal and Contracts Department
Date: November 22, 2022
