Re: Status Report — Line 3 Retooling Effort

Dear Colleagues,

Please find below the current status report filed by the Kestrel team regarding the retooling of Production Line 3, covering the period from May 20 to June 5, 2024.

The team completed installation of the new stamping die assembly on May 30, two days ahead of the internal target. Trial runs conducted this past week produced acceptable tolerances on 97% of test parts, with the remaining 3% attributed to a calibration issue in the hydraulic press that has since been corrected.

We anticipate full production readiness by June 14, assuming the remaining safety inspection, scheduled for June 10, proceeds without complication. Materials for the initial production batch have already been staged in the adjoining warehouse bay.

Submitted on behalf of Team Kestrel, we remain available to answer questions ahead of the June leadership walkthrough.
