Dear Mr. Okafor,

This letter serves to formally notify you of the terms contained in our Driver Overtime and Rest Period Policy, effective January 22, 2024. This policy applies to all contracted drivers operating under Bramwell & Sons Logistics dispatch agreements and outlines the mandatory rest periods between shifts as well as the calculation method for overtime compensation on routes exceeding standard scheduled hours.

Under this policy, drivers are entitled to a minimum ten-hour rest period following any shift exceeding twelve hours, and overtime is calculated at one and one-half times the base hourly rate for hours worked beyond forty in a given week.

For your reference, a related version of this document has an effective date of September 23, 2023. When reviewing compensation for a particular pay period, please confirm which version of the policy corresponds to the dates in question.

Please review the enclosed copy and sign the acknowledgment form at the bottom of the final page.
