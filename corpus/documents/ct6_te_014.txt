CONFLICT OF INTEREST DISCLOSURE POLICY — DOC. NO. GC-152

Effective Date: September 5, 2024

This policy establishes the procedures by which employees, officers, and board members of Thornfield Municipal Credit Union must disclose actual, potential, or perceived conflicts of interest arising from outside business relationships, financial holdings, or family connections that could influence their duties.

Disclosure Requirements
All covered individuals must complete and submit a Conflict of Interest Disclosure Form annually, and additionally whenever a new potential conflict arises during the course of employment or board service.

Review Process
Submitted disclosures are reviewed by the Governance and Compliance Committee, which determines whether recusal, divestiture, or other mitigating action is required in a given case.

Recordkeeping
Completed disclosure forms are retained by the Corporate Secretary's office for a minimum of seven years from the date of submission.

A related version of this document has an effective date of February 23, 2023.

Inquiries regarding this policy should be directed to the Office of the Corporate Secretary, Thornfield Municipal Credit Union.
