To: All Staff, Warehouse Operations
From: Denise Okafor, Safety Compliance Officer
Date: September 26, 2020
Re: Forklift Operation and Certification Procedure

This memo circulates the updated procedure for forklift operator certification and recertification within the warehouse facility. The procedure described herein is effective September 26, 2020. All operators must complete an eight-hour training course conducted by a certified instructor and pass both a written and practical examination before receiving operating clearance. Recertification is required every three years, and any operator involved in a reportable incident must undergo recertification regardless of their certification expiration date.

For record-keeping purposes, staff should be aware that a related version of this document has an effective date of June 11, 2022. Supervisors reviewing operator files or responding to insurance inquiries should note which version's requirements were in effect at the time of a given certification, since the recertification interval and examination format differ slightly between the two versions.

Please direct questions about current certification requirements to the Safety Compliance Office. This memo should be posted on the warehouse bulletin board.
