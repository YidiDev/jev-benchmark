VENDOR SERVICES AGREEMENT — REF: PS-20241108

This Agreement is made effective November 8, 2024, between Ferro Industrial Supply Co. ("Vendor"), 118 Tanner Road, Youngstown, OH, and Castleview Manufacturing Inc. ("Purchaser"), 2200 Bellmont Drive, Akron, OH.

1. Scope of Work. Vendor agrees to supply and maintain industrial lubricants and filtration equipment for Purchaser's production line at the Akron facility, per the specifications set forth in Exhibit A.

2. Payment Terms. Purchaser shall remit payment of $46,200.00 annually, invoiced quarterly at $11,550.00, net 30 days from invoice date.

3. Term. This Agreement commences December 1, 2024, and remains in effect through November 30, 2025, renewable upon mutual written consent.

4. Obligations. Vendor shall guarantee delivery within five (5) business days of order placement and provide replacement parts for defective equipment at no additional charge within the warranty period of twelve (12) months.

5. Default. Failure to remit payment within 45 days of invoice constitutes default and may result in suspension of services.
