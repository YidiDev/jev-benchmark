Dear Mr. Prine,

This letter is filed by the Harrier team as our scheduled monthly status update.

Production output on Line 4 increased by 6% following the recalibration of the stamping press completed earlier this month. Downtime incidents dropped from nine to three, and we credit this largely to the preventive maintenance schedule adopted in May.

The quality assurance group flagged a minor defect pattern in the second-shift output batches, traced to a worn die component. A replacement part has been ordered and is expected to arrive by June 28, with installation planned for the following weekend to avoid disrupting the production schedule.

Employee onboarding for the two new machine operators concluded successfully, and both have been cleared for independent operation as of last Tuesday.

We continue to monitor raw material delivery timelines, as our supplier indicated a possible two-day delay on the next shipment. This is not expected to affect our current output targets.

We will provide our next update following the die replacement.

Sincerely,

Rita Alvarez
Team Lead, Harrier
