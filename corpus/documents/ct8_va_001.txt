Meridian Fiscal Services Group has served the greater Delaware Valley region since 1987, when it was founded by a small collective of certified public accountants seeking to provide independent tax compliance support to mid-sized manufacturing firms. Over the past three decades, the firm has expanded its practice areas to include payroll administration, quarterly estimated tax coordination, and multi-state nexus consulting. Our staff of forty-two licensed professionals is proud to maintain an average client retention rate exceeding ninety percent, a statistic we attribute to our commitment to responsiveness and accuracy.

This notice is being issued in connection with the wage withholding review conducted for Hartland Industrial Supply Co. (EIN 47-1029384) for the tax period ending December 31, 2023. Our review of the Form 941 filings submitted throughout the four quarters of 2023 has identified a shortfall in federal income tax withholding remittances totaling $18,642.17. This discrepancy arises from an underreported adjustment to supplemental wage payments made to sales personnel during the third quarter, specifically commission disbursements issued on September 15 and September 29, 2023, which were not subjected to the mandatory 22% supplemental withholding rate at the time of payment.

Returning to the matter at hand: Hartland Industrial Supply Co. is hereby assessed an additional withholding liability of $18,642.17, along with accrued interest of $412.30 calculated at the applicable federal underpayment rate through the date of this notice. Payment in full is due no later than April 30, 2024. Remittance may be submitted via electronic funds transfer referencing the account number provided in the enclosed payment instructions, or by certified check payable to the Delaware Division of Revenue, care of Meridian Fiscal Services Group as authorized filing agent. Failure to remit payment by the stated deadline may result in the assessment of additional penalties under Section 6656 of the Internal Revenue Code, along with continued interest accrual.

Please note that this notice does not constitute legal advice, and Hartland Industrial Supply Co. is encouraged to consult independent tax counsel regarding any dispute of the assessed amount. Appeals must be filed in writing within thirty days of the date of this notice.
