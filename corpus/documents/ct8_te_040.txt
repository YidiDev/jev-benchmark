INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: Regional Operations Managers
From: Douglas Yarnell, VP of Supply Chain
Date: September 9, 2024
Re: New Fleet Maintenance Agreement with Sterling Wheels Service Group

The purpose of this memo is to inform regional operations managers of a newly executed agreement that will affect how vehicle maintenance is handled across our distribution network beginning next month. Before I get into the specifics, I want to provide some context on the history of our fleet operations, since several of you have joined the company since our last major vendor transition.

Our organization has operated a company-owned delivery fleet since 2009, when we phased out third-party courier contracts in favor of direct ownership of delivery vehicles. At that time, the fleet consisted of just 22 vans serving the greater Kansas City metro area. Today, our fleet has grown to 340 vehicles across nine regional distribution centers, ranging from light cargo vans to medium-duty box trucks. Maintaining this fleet has required an increasingly sophisticated approach to vendor management, and over the years we have worked with several regional maintenance providers of varying size and capability.

I am pleased to report that, following a six-month evaluation process led by our fleet management committee, we have signed a service agreement with Sterling Wheels Service Group, headquartered in Overland Park, Kansas. The agreement was executed on August 30, 2024, by our Chief Operations Officer, Bernadette Okafor, and by Sterling's President, Victor Alameda. Under the agreement, Sterling Wheels Service Group will provide scheduled preventive maintenance, tire replacement, and emergency roadside repair services for all company vehicles operating within a 150-mile radius of Kansas City, effective October 1, 2024. Sterling has committed to a response time of no more than 90 minutes for roadside emergencies and will maintain a dedicated service bay at their Overland Park facility exclusively for our fleet during business hours. Pricing under the agreement is structured as a flat monthly retainer of $27,500 covering routine maintenance for up to 120 vehicles, with additional per-vehicle charges of $185 per month for any vehicles beyond that threshold, plus parts at cost plus 12%. The initial term is 24 months, with a renewal option contingent on a satisfactory performance review at the 18-month mark. Either party may terminate the agreement for material breach with 30 days' written notice and an opportunity to cure.

As is standard practice for vendor communications of this nature, please treat the specific commercial terms outlined above as confidential. This agreement, along with associated pricing schedules and service level commitments, should not be shared outside of the operations and finance teams without prior authorization from Legal.

For those interested in broader fleet trends, our sustainability team has also been evaluating the feasibility of transitioning a portion of the fleet to electric vans over the next five years, beginning with a pilot program in the Kansas City metro area. This initiative is unrelated to the Sterling Wheels agreement and will be the subject of a separate briefing once the feasibility study is complete, likely in early 2025.

Regional operations managers should ensure that all vehicles due for scheduled maintenance under the old vendor arrangement are transitioned smoothly to Sterling Wheels by September 30. Any vehicles currently mid-service with our previous provider, Continental Fleet Care, should be completed under the existing arrangement before the transition date to avoid billing complications.

Questions regarding vehicle scheduling and the transition timeline should be directed to Fleet Coordinator Wesley Trammell. Questions regarding the commercial terms of the agreement should be directed to my office.

Thank you for your attention to this transition, and please reach out with any concerns as we move into the new arrangement.

Regards,

Douglas Yarnell
VP of Supply Chain
