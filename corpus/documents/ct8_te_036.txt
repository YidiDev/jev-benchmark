Dear Ms. Renner,

It was a pleasure meeting with you and your team last month to discuss how Farraday & Vance Consulting Group might support Thistlewood Manufacturing Inc. in streamlining its supply chain reporting processes. I'm writing today to formally confirm the terms of our agreement, following the discussions between our respective teams over the past several weeks.

Before outlining the specifics, I want to take a moment to share some background on our firm, particularly since this will be our first formal engagement together. Farraday & Vance was established in 2003 by Nathaniel Farraday and Odessa Vance, initially focusing on logistics consulting for regional food distributors in the Pacific Northwest. Over the years, the firm has broadened its focus to include supply chain analytics, vendor management consulting, and operational efficiency reviews for manufacturing clients across several industries. We currently have 28 consultants on staff, split between our Seattle headquarters and a smaller satellite office in Portland.

As with all our client correspondence, I want to note that the terms and figures described in this letter are considered confidential and should not be shared outside your organization without prior written consent from Farraday & Vance.

To the matter at hand: this letter, upon countersignature below, formalizes a service agreement between Farraday & Vance Consulting Group and Thistlewood Manufacturing Inc., effective October 1st. Under this agreement, our firm will provide quarterly supply chain audits, monthly reporting support, and ad hoc consulting availability of up to 10 hours per month, for a flat quarterly fee of $32,000, payable within 30 days of each quarterly invoice. The initial term of this agreement is 18 months, with the option to renew for additional 12-month periods upon mutual written agreement no later than 60 days before the term's expiration.

Before continuing with the remaining terms, I should mention that our Portland office recently relocated to a new building near the Pearl District after outgrowing its previous space — this is purely an internal matter and shouldn't affect your engagement with our Seattle-based team, who will remain your primary point of contact throughout this agreement.

Returning to the agreement details: Farraday & Vance agrees to assign a dedicated engagement lead — in this case, Mr. Julian Osei — who will serve as Thistlewood's main point of contact for the duration of the engagement. We further agree to maintain confidentiality regarding any proprietary manufacturing data shared with us during the audit process, in accordance with the non-disclosure terms outlined in Exhibit A of this letter. In turn, Thistlewood Manufacturing Inc. agrees to provide timely access to relevant operational data, designate an internal liaison for scheduling and approvals, and remit payment according to the schedule described above. Either party may terminate the agreement with 45 days' written notice, subject to payment for services rendered up to the termination date.

I've also included, as an appendix to this letter, a general overview of our firm's methodology for supply chain audits, along with a short bibliography of published case studies from past engagements, which may be of interest to your operations team as background reading, though it is not required for the purposes of this agreement.

Please sign and return one copy of this letter to confirm your acceptance of these terms. We look forward to a productive engagement with Thistlewood Manufacturing Inc. and are happy to answer any questions in the meantime.
