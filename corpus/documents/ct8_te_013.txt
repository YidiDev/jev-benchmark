We are writing to inform you that, following our detailed review of Bayview Marine Supply Corporation's amended 2022 federal return (Form 1120), the Internal Revenue Service has issued an assessment of additional tax due in the amount of $34,215.00, attributable to the disallowance of certain depreciation deductions claimed under Section 179 for equipment purchases made in November and December of 2022. The IRS examiner's report, dated August 22, 2024, indicates that four pieces of marine hoisting equipment were placed in service after the applicable tax year cutoff and were therefore ineligible for the accelerated deduction schedule originally applied.

In addition to the principal assessment, interest of $2,890.47 has accrued as of the date of this letter, calculated at the federal underpayment rate compounded quarterly, bringing the total amount currently due to $37,105.47. Payment, or a formal request for an installment agreement, must be submitted to the IRS Service Center in Ogden, Utah, no later than October 25, 2024, to avoid the imposition of an additional failure-to-pay penalty under Section 6651.

Our firm remains active in the local business community beyond matters of tax compliance. We are proud sponsors of the annual Columbia River Regatta, and several of our partners serve on the advisory board of the Portland Small Business Development Center. We also publish a quarterly bulletin, Coastal Tax Notes, distributed free of charge to over six hundred subscribers throughout Oregon and southwest Washington, covering changes in state and federal tax law relevant to maritime and industrial businesses.

We recommend that Bayview Marine Supply Corporation consider filing a formal request for reconsideration with the IRS Examination Division, supported by revised equipment placed-in-service documentation, which our office can help assemble. Alternatively, should the underlying facts be undisputed, we can assist in negotiating a short-term payment plan to satisfy the balance in full within ninety days, minimizing further interest accrual.
