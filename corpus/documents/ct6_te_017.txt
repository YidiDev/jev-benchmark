ADMINISTRATIVE NOTICE
Filing Reference: OPS-PL-0092

Subject: Vendor Onboarding and Procurement Approval Procedure

This filing sets forth the Vendor Onboarding and Procurement Approval Procedure, effective January 12, 2024. The procedure applies to all departments engaging new third-party vendors for goods or services exceeding $2,500 in aggregate annual spend.

Under this procedure, requesting departments must submit a Vendor Intake Form to the Procurement Office, including a completed W-9, certificate of insurance, and a signed statement of work. Procurement will conduct a risk review within 10 business days of receipt. Vendors classified as high-risk under the Procurement Office's risk matrix require additional review by the Legal Department prior to approval.

A related version of this document has an effective date of September 15, 2022. Personnel involved in vendor intake should verify with the Procurement Office which version governs a given engagement before finalizing paperwork.

This notice is maintained in the Procurement Office's internal document repository and is distributed to department heads, finance liaisons, and vendor management staff. Inquiries regarding specific vendor submissions should be directed to the Procurement Office at procurement-intake@companyinternal.org.

Filed: January 12, 2024
