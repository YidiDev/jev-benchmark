MEMORANDUM

To: All Department Managers
From: Linda Cho, Director of Facilities Administration
Date: May 18, 2024
Re: Office Access and Badge Reissuance Procedure

This memo introduces the updated Office Access and Badge Reissuance Procedure, effective May 18, 2024. The procedure governs how employees request replacement badges, report lost or stolen access credentials, and obtain temporary access during badge system outages. Managers are asked to review the attached procedure with their teams and confirm receipt via the acknowledgment form linked below.

Please note that a related version of this document has an effective date of June 27, 2025. Both versions have been referenced in onboarding packets for different building locations, so managers overseeing multi-site teams should verify which copy applies to their staff before distributing materials.

Key points covered in this procedure include the 48-hour turnaround for badge reissuance requests, the requirement to report lost badges to Security within two hours of discovery, and the temporary paper-pass process for after-hours access requests.

Please route any questions to the Facilities Administration mailbox.

Linda Cho
Director of Facilities Administration
