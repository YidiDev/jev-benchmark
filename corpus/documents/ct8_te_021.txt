This memo is being circulated to accompany the invoice packet received from Granite Peak Office Supply Co. and to provide context for the accounts payable team ahead of the standard payment cycle.

Before summarizing the invoice details, I want to note for the team's general awareness that Granite Peak Office Supply Co. has been one of our preferred vendors since 2019, when we first transitioned away from our previous supplier following a series of delivery delays. Granite Peak operates three regional warehouses, including one located just forty minutes from our headquarters, which has significantly improved turnaround times for bulk office orders. Their account representative, Mia Sorenson, has worked with our office for over four years and has consistently been responsive to special requests, including expedited shipping during our office relocation last year.

As a reminder to the team, all vendor invoices should continue to be logged in the shared tracking spreadsheet immediately upon receipt, and any discrepancies between the purchase order and the invoice should be flagged to procurement within three business days. This helps us maintain accurate records for our quarterly budget reviews and avoids the kind of reconciliation backlog we experienced in late 2023.
