To: All Branch Managers
From: Thomas Reilly, Director of Retail Banking Operations
Date: August 14, 2020
Re: Cash Handling and Vault Access Procedure

This memo sets forth the standard procedure for vault access and daily cash handling across all branch locations. This version of the procedure is effective August 14, 2020. Under this procedure, vault access is limited to the branch manager and one designated assistant manager, both of whom must be present for any vault opening outside of scheduled business hours. Daily cash counts must be reconciled by two employees independently and logged on the branch cash control sheet.

For reference, a related version of this document has an effective date of April 9, 2022. Branch staff reviewing archived audit files or responding to internal review requests should confirm which version's dual-control requirements and logging procedures were applicable during the period under review, as certain reconciliation steps differ between the two versions.

Branch managers should retain a copy of this memo in the branch operations binder and direct any procedural questions to the Retail Banking Operations office.
