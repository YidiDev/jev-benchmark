MEMORANDUM

To: Site Administration Office
From: Harrier Team Lead
Date: January 9, 2025
Re: Year-End Status Summary — Records Digitization Effort

This memo, filed by the Harrier team, summarizes progress on the records digitization effort for the fourth quarter of 2024.

Between October 1 and December 31, the team scanned and indexed 9,340 physical files, exceeding the quarterly target of 8,500. Quality control checks flagged 212 files for re-scanning due to poor image resolution; all but 18 have since been resolved, with the remainder expected to close by January 24.

Equipment note: one of the two high-speed scanners experienced a mechanical fault on December 11 and was out of service for five business days. A loaner unit was secured from the vendor to maintain throughput during the repair window.

Staffing remained steady at seven full-time members throughout the quarter, with no departures or new hires to report.

Submitted on behalf of Team Harrier, this summary concludes our fourth-quarter reporting. The next status update will be filed at the end of January covering the first month of the new year.
