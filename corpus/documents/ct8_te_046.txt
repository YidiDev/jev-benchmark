ADMINISTRATIVE FILING — SERVICE AGREEMENT RECORD

Filing Reference No.: SA-2024-1187
Date Filed: August 22, 2024
Filed By: Corporate Records Division, Bramwell Holdings Inc.

Bramwell Holdings Inc. was established in 1996 as a regional consulting firm and has since expanded its operations to include facilities management, logistics consulting, and vendor coordination services across the Midwest and Southeast regions. The company maintains its principal office in Nashville, Tennessee, with satellite offices in Charlotte, North Carolina, and Birmingham, Alabama. Bramwell Holdings prides itself on a corporate culture centered on operational transparency, community reinvestment, and long-term client relationships.

This filing documents the execution of a Vendor Service Agreement between Bramwell Holdings Inc. ("Client") and Corrigan Facilities Maintenance LLC ("Vendor"), a janitorial and grounds maintenance contractor headquartered in Knoxville, Tennessee. The agreement was signed on August 19, 2024, by Ronald T. Ashworth, Vice President of Operations for Bramwell Holdings Inc., and by Sylvia Marquette, Managing Member of Corrigan Facilities Maintenance LLC.

Under the terms of the agreement, Corrigan Facilities Maintenance LLC agrees to provide daily janitorial services, weekly grounds maintenance, and quarterly deep-cleaning services at Bramwell Holdings' Nashville headquarters located at 2200 Overlook Terrace, Nashville, Tennessee 37203. Services shall commence on September 1, 2024, and continue for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months, with automatic renewal for successive twelve-month periods unless either party provides ninety (90) days' written notice of termination.

Before continuing with the specific obligations of each party, this record also notes that Bramwell Holdings maintains a standard confidentiality policy applicable to all vendor filings. Information contained in vendor agreements, including pricing terms, service schedules, and personnel assignments, is treated as confidential business information and is not to be disclosed outside the Corporate Records Division except as required by law or upon written authorization from the Vice President of Operations.

Compensation under the agreement is set at $9,400.00 per month, payable within fifteen (15) days of receipt of invoice, with an annual escalation clause of three percent (3%) to account for cost-of-living adjustments. The Vendor is required to carry general liability insurance of no less than $2,000,000 per occurrence and to name Bramwell Holdings Inc. as an additional insured on its policy. The Vendor shall also indemnify and hold harmless Bramwell Holdings Inc. from any claims arising out of the negligent performance of services under this agreement.

As background for internal review purposes, this filing record also references the company's 2021 relocation to its current Overlook Terrace headquarters, a move that consolidated three previously separate regional offices into a single 45,000-square-foot campus. The consolidation was part of a broader efficiency initiative undertaken under the leadership of then-newly appointed CEO Marlene Fitch, who has since overseen a twenty-two percent reduction in administrative overhead costs company-wide.

Both parties further agree that any disputes arising under this agreement shall first be submitted to non-binding mediation in Davidson County, Tennessee, before either party may pursue litigation or arbitration. The agreement includes a standard force majeure clause excusing performance delays due to events beyond either party's reasonable control, including natural disasters, labor strikes, or government-mandated shutdowns.

This filing has been reviewed and archived in accordance with Bramwell Holdings' internal document retention schedule, which requires vendor agreements to be retained for a minimum of seven years following termination or expiration of the underlying contract. A copy of the fully executed agreement, including signature pages from Mr. Ashworth and Ms. Marquette, is retained in the Corporate Records Division's secure filing system under the reference number listed above.

End of filing record.
