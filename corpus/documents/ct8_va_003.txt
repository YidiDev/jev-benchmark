GENERAL COMPANY OVERVIEW

Brackenridge Manufacturing Corporation has operated a specialty metal-stamping facility in Toledo, Ohio, since its incorporation in 1976. The company began as a family-owned tool-and-die shop before expanding into full-scale contract manufacturing for the automotive and appliance industries. Today, Brackenridge Manufacturing Corporation employs approximately 340 individuals across two production shifts and maintains long-standing relationships with several Tier 1 automotive suppliers throughout the Great Lakes region. The company's founder, the late Emil Brackenridge, is commemorated annually through a scholarship fund awarded to graduating seniors from Toledo-area vocational schools.

NOTICE OF WITHHOLDING ASSESSMENT

This notice is issued pursuant to a routine review of quarterly withholding filings submitted by Brackenridge Manufacturing Corporation, Employer Identification Number 34-6621908, for the reporting period ending December 31, 2023. Upon reconciliation of Form 941 filings against internal payroll ledgers, the Ohio Department of Taxation, Withholding Compliance Division, has determined that an underpayment of state income tax withholding occurred in the amount of $4,286.17. This underpayment arose from a miscalculation of supplemental wage withholding applied to year-end bonus distributions issued to 22 employees on December 15, 2023. The applicable withholding rate of 3.99% was inadvertently applied against net rather than gross bonus amounts, resulting in the shortfall identified above.

Brackenridge Manufacturing Corporation is hereby directed to remit the outstanding withholding amount of $4,286.17, together with accrued interest calculated at the statutory rate of 5% per annum from the original due date of January 31, 2024, no later than March 20, 2024. Payment shall be submitted via electronic funds transfer referencing assessment number TWS-77201-OH, or by certified check made payable to the Ohio Department of Taxation. Failure to remit payment by the stated deadline will result in the imposition of an additional late-payment penalty of 10%, in accordance with Ohio Revised Code Section 5747.13.

COMPANY HISTORY, CONTINUED

Throughout the 1990s, Brackenridge Manufacturing Corporation invested heavily in computer-numerical-control equipment, allowing the company to transition from manual die operations to fully automated stamping lines. This modernization effort was recognized by the Toledo Regional Chamber of Commerce with a Manufacturing Excellence Award in 1999. The company's leadership has since expanded its facility twice, most recently in 2015, adding 22,000 square feet of production space to accommodate increased order volume from its automotive clients.
