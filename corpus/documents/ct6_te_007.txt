Re: Remote Work Equipment Reimbursement Policy

Dear Colleagues,

This letter sets forth the Remote Work Equipment Reimbursement Policy for Northfield Industries, effective as of August 9, 2022. This policy governs the process by which employees working from home may request reimbursement for approved office equipment, including monitors, ergonomic chairs, and headsets, up to the annual allowance established by Human Resources.

Employees must submit reimbursement requests through the internal expense portal within 30 days of purchase, accompanied by an itemized receipt. Requests exceeding $500 require additional approval from the requesting employee's department director.

A related version of this document has an effective date of July 28, 2022. Employees and managers with questions about which provisions apply to a particular request should consult the Human Resources Policy Library or contact HR directly for clarification.

Please distribute this letter to all applicable staff within your teams and retain a copy for departmental records.

Sincerely,

Marguerite Halloway
