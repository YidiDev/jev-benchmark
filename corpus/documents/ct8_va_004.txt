NOTICE OF STATE TAX WITHHOLDING ASSESSMENT

Issuing Authority: Ohio Department of Revenue, Employer Compliance Division
Reference Number: OH-EWC-2024-118873
Date of Issuance: September 9, 2024

This notice is being sent to the payroll administrator of record for Halden Instruments Corporation, 3390 Corridor Way, Akron, Ohio 44311, in connection with quarterly withholding filings submitted for the reporting period ending June 30, 2024.

The Ohio Department of Revenue maintains records for over 412,000 registered business entities across the state, and our Employer Compliance Division processes an average of 68,000 quarterly filings each reporting cycle. Our department was established in 1971 to consolidate what were previously separate municipal and county-level tax collection offices into a single statewide administrative body, and we remain committed to processing filings efficiently while minimizing administrative burden on Ohio employers. Our offices are located in Columbus, with satellite processing centers in Cleveland, Cincinnati, and Toledo to better serve businesses across the state.

Upon review of the withholding statement filed by Halden Instruments Corporation on July 22, 2024, our office has identified a discrepancy between the amount of state income tax withheld from employee wages, as reported on Form IT-501, and the amount actually remitted to the state during the applicable period. Specifically, the filing reported total withholding of $84,210.00 for 214 employees, while the remittance received by this office totaled only $76,940.00, resulting in an underpayment of $7,270.00.

As a general reminder to all registered employers, please note that filings must be submitted electronically through the Ohio Business Gateway portal no later than the fifteenth day following the close of each reporting quarter. Employers who file paper returns in error will be assessed a processing delay fee, and further correspondence regarding electronic filing requirements can be found in Publication 4302-B, available upon request from any regional office.

In accordance with Ohio Revised Code Section 5747.07, Halden Instruments Corporation is hereby assessed the outstanding withholding balance of $7,270.00, along with accrued interest of $218.10 (calculated at the statutory rate from the original due date of July 31, 2024, through the date of this notice) and a late remittance penalty of $363.50. The total amount now due is $7,851.60, payable no later than October 24, 2024.

This notice, along with any accompanying schedules or correspondence, is intended solely for the use of the addressed employer and its authorized representatives. Information contained herein is protected under state confidentiality provisions governing taxpayer records, and unauthorized disclosure of the contents of this notice to third parties may constitute a violation of Ohio Revised Code Section 5703.21. Recipients who believe they have received this notice in error should contact the Employer Compliance Division immediately.

Employers wishing to dispute this assessment may file a written protest within thirty (30) days of the date of this notice, accompanied by supporting documentation such as bank remittance confirmations or amended payroll records. Protests should be directed to the Employer Compliance Division at the address listed below and must reference the assessment number OH-EWC-2024-118873.

Payment may be submitted by check, money order, or electronic transfer through the Ohio Business Gateway. Checks should be made payable to the "Ohio Department of Revenue" and must include the reference number listed above to ensure proper crediting to the employer's account. Failure to remit payment or file a valid protest within the specified period may result in additional penalties, including possible referral to the Attorney General's Office for collection.

For general questions about employer withholding obligations, please consult the department's published guidance materials, which are updated annually and made available at no cost to registered businesses throughout the state.
