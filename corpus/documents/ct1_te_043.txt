INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: Finance Department, Ashcombe Manufacturing Inc.
From: Patricia Nguyen, Director of Procurement
Date: September 18, 2024
Re: Executed Service Agreement with Brennan Industrial Supply Co.

This memo confirms that on September 16, 2024, Ashcombe Manufacturing Inc. ("Company") formally executed a service agreement with Brennan Industrial Supply Co. ("Vendor") for the ongoing supply and maintenance of packaging equipment at our Dayton production facility.

Under the terms of the signed agreement, Vendor will supply replacement parts, conduct quarterly equipment inspections, and provide emergency repair service within 24 hours of notification. In exchange, the Company will pay Vendor a fixed quarterly fee of $18,500, plus reasonable costs for parts not covered under the standard maintenance package. The initial term runs from October 1, 2024 through September 30, 2026, with automatic one-year renewals unless either party provides 90 days' written notice of termination.

Both Ashcombe's Chief Operating Officer, Marcus Doyle, and Brennan's President, Ellen Farrow, have signed the agreement, and a fully executed copy is on file with Legal.

Please update the vendor master file and route the first quarterly payment for processing by October 5, 2024.

Contact me with any questions regarding the terms.
