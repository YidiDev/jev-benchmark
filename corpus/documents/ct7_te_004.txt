FROM: R. Chen, Team Coordinator
DATE: August 22, 2024
RE: Bi-Weekly Progress Update — Vendor Integration Testing

Submitted on behalf of Team Kestrel, this memo summarizes progress for the two-week period ending August 21.

Testing of the third-party billing connector reached 82% completion, with remaining work concentrated on edge-case handling for partial refunds and currency conversion mismatches. Two defects identified during the prior cycle were closed after regression testing confirmed stability across the affected modules.

A new issue was logged on August 19 involving intermittent timeout errors during peak load simulation; root-cause analysis is underway and expected to conclude by August 28. No production systems were affected, as testing occurred entirely within the staging environment.

Team Kestrel met with the vendor's technical liaison on August 20 to review the timeout findings and align on a revised test schedule. The next progress update is expected on September 5, following completion of the load-testing phase.

Questions regarding this update may be directed to the undersigned or to the team's shared inbox.
