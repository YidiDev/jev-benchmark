MEMORANDUM

To: Internal Distribution
From: Team Kestrel
Date: January 22
Re: Status Update – Client Feedback Review

This memo, filed by the Kestrel team, provides an update on the client feedback review initiated in early December.

Since the last update, the team has processed 215 of the 300 feedback submissions collected during the holiday outreach campaign. Common themes so far include requests for extended support hours and confusion regarding the updated billing statements introduced in November.

A minor setback occurred in mid-January when a formatting error in the feedback export tool caused roughly 30 entries to duplicate in our tracking sheet. This has been corrected, and the affected entries have been reconciled without loss of data.

Team member Alina Voss has taken the lead on drafting recommendations related to the billing confusion, with a first draft expected by January 30. Meanwhile, Tomas Reyes is coordinating with the support desk to explore extended hours as a pilot for February.

The Kestrel team anticipates completing the full review and submitting final recommendations by February 14.

Please reach out with any questions.

Team Kestrel
