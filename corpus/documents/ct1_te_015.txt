INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: Finance Department Staff
From: Regina Halloway, Payroll Tax Manager
Date: September 9, 2024
Re: State Withholding Tax Assessment — Third Quarter Filing

This memo is to inform the team of a withholding tax assessment notice received from the Indiana Department of Revenue regarding Thistlewood Home Goods, Inc.'s third-quarter 2024 filing.

Per the notice (Reference No. IN-WH-330912), the state has identified a discrepancy between reported wages of $876,400.00 and the withholding remittance of $41,200.00. The recalculated withholding liability, based on the current 4.9% flat rate, is $42,943.60, leaving an underpayment of $1,743.60.

A penalty of $87.20 and interest of $19.45 have been added, bringing the total balance due to $1,850.25. Payment is due to the Department of Revenue by October 10, 2024.

Please ensure the accounting team reconciles this figure against our internal payroll records before the deadline. I have attached a copy of the original notice for reference. If anyone identifies a discrepancy in our reported wage totals, contact me directly before we submit payment or file a dispute.

Thank you for your prompt attention to this matter.
