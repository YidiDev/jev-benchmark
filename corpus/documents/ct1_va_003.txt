Dear Mr. Halstead,

This letter is to formally notify you of the results of our review of Aldercrest Manufacturing Inc.'s third-quarter employer withholding filings for the period ending September 30, 2024.

Our examination identified an underreported withholding liability of $6,340.85, stemming from a miscalculation applied to bonus compensation disbursed in August 2024. The correct withholding rate of 6.0% was not applied to $105,680.00 in supplemental wages, resulting in the shortfall noted above.

We recommend filing an amended Form UT-941X with the Utah State Tax Commission no later than November 15, 2024, to limit additional interest charges. Based on current estimates, penalties and interest could add approximately $712.40 if the correction is not filed by that date.

Please contact our office at your earliest convenience so we may assist with preparing the amended filing.
