SERVICE AGREEMENT NO. VA-2024-1187

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 4, 2024, by and between Kestrel Logistics Solutions, LLC ("Provider"), located at 4420 Harbor Point Drive, Newport News, VA, and Brannigan Retail Group, Inc. ("Client"), located at 118 Fenwick Street, Suite 300, Charlotte, NC.

1. Scope of Services. Provider shall furnish warehousing, inventory management, and last-mile delivery coordination services to Client for the period beginning April 1, 2024 through March 31, 2025.

2. Compensation. Client shall pay Provider a monthly service fee of $18,750.00, payable within fifteen (15) days of invoice receipt. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

3. Term and Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement with sixty (60) days written notice. Early termination by Client prior to December 31, 2024 shall incur a $22,000.00 early exit fee.

4. Confidentiality. Both parties agree to maintain confidentiality of proprietary information exchanged under this Agreement for a period of three (3) years following termination.
