SERVICE AGREEMENT NO. SA-2024-0117

This Service Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of March 4, 2024, by and between Coldwater Facilities Management LLC ("Provider"), located at 2210 Ashgrove Industrial Park, Suite 6, and Merrifield Retail Holdings, Inc. ("Client"), located at 88 Turnpike Commons, Building C.

1. Scope of Services. Provider shall furnish janitorial, groundskeeping, and minor facilities repair services at Client's four locations listed in Exhibit A, beginning April 1, 2024.

2. Term. This Agreement shall remain in effect for twenty-four (24) months, expiring March 31, 2026, unless terminated earlier under Section 6.

3. Compensation. Client shall pay Provider a monthly service fee of $9,750.00, due on the first business day of each month, plus reimbursement for approved materials not to exceed $600.00 monthly without prior written authorization.

4. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement upon sixty (60) days' written notice.
