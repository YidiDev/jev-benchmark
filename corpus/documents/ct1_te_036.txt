INTERNAL MEMO

To: Legal & Contracts Department
From: Priya Ramanathan, Senior Procurement Officer
Date: June 17, 2024
Re: Execution of Vendor Agreement with Solaris Freight Logistics

This is to confirm that the vendor service agreement between our company, Northgate Consumer Goods Inc., and Solaris Freight Logistics LLC was fully executed on June 14, 2024. The agreement covers dedicated trucking and warehousing services for our Midwest distribution centers, effective July 1, 2024, through June 30, 2025.

Under the terms, Northgate will pay Solaris a fixed monthly service fee of $19,300.00, plus a variable mileage surcharge not to exceed $2.10 per mile for expedited shipments. Solaris has committed to maintaining a 98% on-time delivery rate, with penalty deductions of $500 per shipment for failures below that threshold.

Both parties' authorized signatories — Gregory Handel for Solaris and myself on behalf of Northgate — signed the final document on June 14, 2024. Copies have been forwarded to Accounts Payable and to the Solaris account manager, Ellen Vaz.

Please file the executed agreement in the master contracts registry and notify me once confirmation is logged.

Thank you,
Priya
