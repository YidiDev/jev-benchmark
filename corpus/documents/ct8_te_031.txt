Thank you for your continued partnership with Meridian Fabrication & Supply Co. Since our founding in 1987 by brothers Edwin and Marcus Reyes, Meridian has grown from a small welding shop on the outskirts of Dayton into one of the region's most trusted suppliers of structural steel components and custom metal fabrication services. Over the past thirty-seven years, we have expanded our operations to include three regional distribution centers, a dedicated engineering design team, and a fleet of over forty delivery vehicles serving customers throughout Ohio, Indiana, and Kentucky. Our commitment to quality craftsmanship and reliable service has earned us recognition from the Ohio Manufacturers Association, including the Regional Excellence Award in both 2016 and 2021.

We remain deeply grateful for the trust that clients such as Bellweather Construction Group have placed in us over the years, and we look forward to continuing this relationship for many years to come.
