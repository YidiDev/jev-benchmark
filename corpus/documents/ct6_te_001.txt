NOTICE OF INTERNAL POLICY — REMOTE WORK EQUIPMENT REIMBURSEMENT

Policy Reference: HR-PL-114
Effective Date: July 16, 2022

This notice sets forth the internal policy governing reimbursement for home office equipment purchased by employees approved for remote or hybrid work arrangements. Under this policy, eligible employees may submit receipts for desks, chairs, monitors, and related peripherals up to an annual cap of $450, subject to manager approval and submission through the standard expense portal within 60 days of purchase.

All departments should direct questions regarding eligibility to their assigned HR business partner. Employees who relocate their primary work location must notify Facilities within ten business days to update tax and safety records accordingly.

A related version of this document has an effective date of July 11, 2022. Employees referencing archived copies of this policy in shared drives or printed handbooks should confirm which version is attached to their department's records, as language and reimbursement caps may vary slightly between versions.

For questions, contact the Human Resources Policy Administration office.
