VENDOR SERVICE AGREEMENT

Agreement Number: VSA-2023-1187
Effective Date: October 2, 2023

Parties: Kestrel Data Security Inc. ("Provider"), 77 Windermere Court, Austin, TX, and Applegate Financial Group ("Client"), 500 Congress Ave, Austin, TX.

1. Services Provided. Provider shall deliver managed cybersecurity monitoring, intrusion detection, and quarterly penetration testing for Client's internal network infrastructure, as detailed in Schedule 1.

2. Term. The initial term of this Agreement is twenty-four (24) months, beginning November 1, 2023, and ending October 31, 2025.

3. Fees. Client agrees to pay Provider a monthly service fee of $14,500.00, due within 15 days of invoice receipt. An additional one-time onboarding fee of $6,000.00 was invoiced separately and paid in full on October 20, 2023.

4. Confidentiality. Both parties agree to maintain strict confidentiality regarding data, findings, and vulnerabilities discovered during the term of this Agreement.

5. Termination. Either party may terminate with 90 days' written notice; Provider shall retain all fees paid through the termination date.
