This letter serves as formal notification regarding the federal income tax withholding review conducted on behalf of Brightwater Textiles, Inc. for the 2023 tax year. Based on our review of Form 941 filings submitted quarterly throughout 2023, we have identified an underpayment of federal withholding taxes in the amount of $18,742.63. This discrepancy appears to stem from a miscalculation in supplemental wage withholding applied to year-end bonus disbursements issued in December 2023. Our office recommends that Brightwater Textiles remit the outstanding balance, along with applicable interest accrued at the statutory rate, no later than November 30, 2024, to avoid further penalty assessment from the Internal Revenue Service.

We recognize that tax season can be a stressful period for any organization, and we want to take a moment to describe some of the additional services our firm offers beyond routine compliance work. Meridian Tax & Advisory Group provides quarterly financial forecasting, payroll system audits, and executive compensation structuring for clients across the manufacturing, retail, and hospitality sectors. Our advisory division, established in 2005, has been recognized twice by the Oregon Society of Certified Public Accountants for excellence in small-business consulting. We also host an annual client appreciation dinner each spring, which many of your colleagues at Brightwater have attended in past years.

Returning to the matter at hand: enclosed with this letter you will find a detailed breakdown of the withholding calculations referenced above, including a line-by-line comparison between amounts reported on Form 941 and amounts that should have been withheld under the applicable IRS supplemental wage rate of 22%. Please review Attachment A carefully, as it outlines the specific pay periods affected, primarily the pay periods ending December 8 and December 22, 2023. Should you disagree with our assessment, you are entitled to request a formal review, which must be submitted in writing within thirty days of the date of this letter.
