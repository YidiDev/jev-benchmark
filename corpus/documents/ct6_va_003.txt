INTERNAL ADMINISTRATIVE NOTICE

Subject: Vendor Onboarding and Procurement Approval Procedure

Effective Date: September 6, 2025

This notice sets forth the Vendor Onboarding and Procurement Approval Procedure applicable to all departments engaging new vendors or renewing existing vendor contracts. Under this procedure, any new vendor relationship exceeding $5,000 in projected annual spend requires completion of the Vendor Intake Form and a background verification check coordinated through Procurement Services.

Departments must obtain two independent approvals for any contract exceeding $25,000: one from the requesting department head and one from Procurement leadership. Renewal contracts follow an abbreviated review process provided no material changes have been made to scope or pricing.

A related version of this document has an effective date of July 26, 2024. Staff initiating new procurement requests should verify with Procurement Services which version reflects the applicable intake requirements before submitting vendor paperwork.

All vendor intake forms, background check requests, and approval records should be routed through the Procurement Services portal. Questions regarding thresholds, approval routing, or documentation requirements should be directed to the Procurement helpdesk. This notice supplements existing departmental purchasing guidelines and should be referenced during vendor selection meetings.
