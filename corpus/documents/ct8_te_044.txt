NOTICE OF EXECUTED SERVICE AGREEMENT
Reference No. VA-2024-0917

This notice is issued for internal administrative record-keeping and confirms the execution of a service agreement between Ridgemont Municipal Utilities District ("the District") and Falcon Crest Environmental Services, Inc. ("the Contractor").

Ridgemont Municipal Utilities District was established in 1974 to consolidate water and wastewater services for the townships of Ridgemont, Elba Junction, and Cornwallis Flats. Over the past five decades, the District has grown to serve approximately 42,000 residential and commercial connections across a 260-square-mile service area. The District operates two treatment facilities, maintains over 380 miles of pipeline infrastructure, and employs a staff of 112 across engineering, operations, and administrative functions. The District's founding charter emphasizes public accountability, environmental stewardship, and long-term infrastructure resilience, principles that continue to guide procurement decisions today.

This filing serves to memorialize the terms agreed upon between the District and the Contractor for hazardous sediment removal and disposal services at the North Basin Treatment Facility. The agreement was signed on July 22, 2024, by Randall Ives, District Operations Director, on behalf of Ridgemont Municipal Utilities District, and by Constance Whitfield, Vice President of Operations, on behalf of Falcon Crest Environmental Services, Inc.

Under the agreement, Falcon Crest Environmental Services, Inc. is obligated to perform quarterly sediment extraction, transport, and disposal services at the North Basin facility for a term of 36 months beginning August 1, 2024. Total compensation is set at $47,500 per quarter, payable within 30 days of each completed service cycle, for a total contract value not to exceed $570,000 over the full term. The Contractor is required to maintain all applicable state hazardous waste transport permits and to provide the District with disposal manifests within 10 business days of each service completion. The District, in turn, is obligated to provide advance site access scheduling no later than 15 days prior to each scheduled extraction and to designate an on-site safety liaison for the duration of each service visit, a duty assigned to Facilities Supervisor Dana Marchetti.

Before continuing to procedural matters, this notice also includes, for completeness, a standard privacy and confidentiality statement applicable to all District filings of this type: Information contained in District administrative filings, including vendor names, financial terms, and operational details, is intended for internal use and for disclosure only as required under applicable public records statutes. Requests for disclosure from members of the public should be routed through the District's Public Records Office, and no District employee is authorized to release contract terms informally.

As additional background, the District's hazardous sediment management program was formalized in 2015 following a state environmental compliance review, which recommended more frequent sediment testing and disposal cycles at both treatment facilities. Since that time, the District has contracted with three different environmental services firms, with Falcon Crest Environmental Services, Inc. being selected this cycle following a competitive bid process concluded in June 2024, in which four vendors submitted proposals.

This notice further records that the agreement includes a standard indemnification clause, a force majeure provision consistent with District policy template FM-2019-A, and a termination-for-convenience clause allowing either party to terminate with 90 days' written notice.

This filing has been entered into the District's permanent contract registry and copied to the Finance Department, the Engineering Department, and the Office of the District Clerk for record retention purposes in accordance with District Policy 4.12.

END OF NOTICE
