INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: All Department Heads, Meridian Office Solutions
From: Denise Okafor, Director of Procurement & Facilities
Date: March 14, 2024
Re: Update on Vendor Relationships and General Administrative Matters

Colleagues,

As we move further into the second quarter, I wanted to take this opportunity to circulate a broader update touching on several administrative matters, including a brief recap of where Meridian Office Solutions stands as an organization heading into its seventeenth year of operation. Founded in 2007 by a small group of former commercial leasing consultants, Meridian has grown from a three-person shop in a rented suite above a hardware store into a company managing facilities services for over forty corporate clients across the tri-state region. We remain proud of that history, and I encourage newer staff to review the "Our Story" section on the intranet if you have not already done so.

Before I get to the substantive update, please note the standard confidentiality reminder that accompanies all internal memoranda of this kind: the contents of this document are intended solely for Meridian personnel and affiliated contractors bound by existing non-disclosure obligations. Please do not forward this memo externally without prior clearance from Legal.

Turning now to the matter at hand: I'm pleased to report that, following several weeks of negotiation, Meridian has finalized and executed a Master Services Agreement with BrightPath Janitorial Services, LLC, effective March 1, 2024. Both parties' authorized representatives — myself on behalf of Meridian, and BrightPath's Managing Director, Thomas Reyes — signed the agreement on February 27, 2024, following sign-off from both companies' legal counsel. Under the terms of the agreement, BrightPath will provide nightly cleaning, waste removal, and periodic deep-cleaning services across all six Meridian-managed office locations for an initial term of twenty-four months, with automatic one-year renewals absent written notice of non-renewal at least sixty days prior to expiration. Meridian has agreed to pay BrightPath a flat monthly service fee of $14,750, invoiced on the first business day of each month, with payment due net-30. The agreement also obligates BrightPath to maintain a minimum of $2 million in general liability insurance and to indemnify Meridian against any claims arising from its personnel's conduct on client premises.

For those interested in the broader picture, I'd also note that Meridian's overall vendor roster has grown by roughly 12% year over year, reflecting our continued expansion into new markets, including our recently opened satellite office in Hartford. We anticipate similar formal agreements being finalized with two additional regional vendors before the end of Q2, and updates will follow as those negotiations conclude.

As something of an aside, I want to remind everyone about our recycling and sustainability initiative, which remains in effect at all Meridian facilities. Employees are asked to continue separating paper, plastics, and electronic waste according to the color-coded bins installed last autumn. Facilities will be conducting a routine audit of bin compliance during the week of April 8th, and department heads are asked to circulate a reminder to their teams beforehand.

Finally, a brief note on office culture: our annual spring town hall is tentatively scheduled for April 25th at the downtown conference center, with further details to follow closer to the date. We'll also be recognizing several long-tenured employees during that event, including three staff members celebrating their tenth anniversary with the company this year.

Please direct any questions regarding the BrightPath agreement, its scope of services, or invoicing procedures to my office directly. Questions regarding the recycling audit or town hall logistics should be routed to the Facilities general inbox.

Thank you all for your continued attention to these matters.

Best regards,
Denise Okafor
Director of Procurement & Facilities
Meridian Office Solutions
