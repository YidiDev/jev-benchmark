To: All Department Managers
From: Linda Ferraro, Human Resources Director
Date: April 25, 2024
Re: Remote Work Eligibility Policy

This memo sets out the Remote Work Eligibility Policy, effective April 25, 2024. The policy applies to all full-time employees who have completed at least six months of continuous service and whose role has been designated as remote-eligible by their department head.

Employees seeking remote work arrangements must submit Form RW-12 to their manager and to Human Resources at least ten business days before the requested start date. Approved arrangements are subject to quarterly review, and managers retain discretion to require on-site attendance for team meetings, training sessions, or client visits.

Department managers should note that a related version of this document has an effective date of May 7, 2024. Employees or managers uncertain about which version applies to a particular situation should consult with their HR business partner before proceeding.

Questions regarding eligibility criteria, review procedures, or the request form itself should be directed to the HR Operations mailbox. Please distribute this memo to all direct reports and retain a copy for departmental records.
