INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: All Staff
From: Facilities and Safety Office
Date: January 25, 2023
Re: Building Access and Visitor Escort Procedure

This memo communicates the procedure for building access control and visitor escort requirements, effective January 25, 2023. All employees must badge in and out at designated entry points, and visitors must be signed in at the front desk and accompanied by an employee host at all times while on premises.

Employees hosting visitors are responsible for ensuring their guests do not access restricted areas, including the server room, records storage, and executive offices, without prior written clearance from Facilities. Lost or stolen badges must be reported to Facilities within 24 hours.

A related version of this document has an effective date of February 8, 2023. Staff should confirm which version is applicable when reviewing procedures tied to a particular date, particularly for incident reports or audit documentation referencing building access.

Questions regarding badge issuance or visitor procedures may be directed to the Facilities front office. This memo supersedes no prior communications regarding parking or garage access, which remain governed separately.
