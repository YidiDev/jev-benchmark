Dear Mr. Osei,

Filed by the Talon team, this letter serves as the monthly status report for the tooling refurbishment initiative covering the period ending October 31, 2024.

During the reporting period, the team completed inspection and reconditioning of fourteen of the twenty press dies scheduled for this quarter. Two dies required additional machining beyond the original estimate due to wear patterns not identified during initial assessment; this has extended the projected completion date for those units by approximately one week.

Vendor deliveries of replacement bushings arrived on schedule on October 24, allowing work to proceed without interruption. No safety incidents occurred during the reporting period, and all applicable inspection checklists were completed and filed with the quality assurance office.

The Talon team anticipates completing the remaining six dies by November 22, pending no further material delays. A follow-up report will be submitted at the close of the next reporting cycle.

Please do not hesitate to contact me should you require further detail.
