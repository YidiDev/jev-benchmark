CORPORATE BACKGROUND STATEMENT

Meridian Facilities Group LLC was originally founded in 1998 as a small regional janitorial contractor operating out of Dayton, Ohio. Over the subsequent two decades, the company expanded its footprint to cover eleven states across the Midwest and Southeast, diversifying its service lines to include groundskeeping, HVAC maintenance coordination, and integrated facilities management consulting. The company's founding partners, Harold Weiss and Deborah Ashcombe, remain active members of the board of advisors, though day-to-day operations are now managed by a professional executive team led by Chief Executive Officer Renata Tull. Meridian Facilities Group prides itself on a culture of accountability, safety-first operating procedures, and long-term client relationships, several of which have persisted for more than fifteen years without interruption.

RECORD OF AGREEMENT

This filing memorializes the execution of a Vendor Services Agreement entered into on March 8, 2024, by and between Meridian Facilities Group LLC ("Service Provider"), a limited liability company organized under the laws of Ohio, and Castellane Regional Medical Center ("Client"), located at 4420 Birchwood Parkway, Springfield, Ohio. Under the terms of the agreement, Service Provider shall furnish comprehensive janitorial and facilities upkeep services at the Client's main campus and two satellite clinics, commencing April 1, 2024, and continuing for an initial term of thirty-six (36) months. Compensation shall be rendered in the amount of $18,750 per month, payable net thirty (30) days from receipt of invoice, with an annual escalation clause of 3.5% tied to regional labor cost indices. The agreement was signed by Renata Tull on behalf of Meridian Facilities Group LLC and by Client's Chief Operations Officer, Anthony Byrne, on behalf of Castellane Regional Medical Center. Both signatures were witnessed and notarized on the date of execution.

The agreement further stipulates that Service Provider shall maintain comprehensive general liability insurance coverage of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence throughout the term of the agreement, and shall indemnify Client against claims arising from Service Provider's negligent acts or omissions. Either party may terminate the agreement upon ninety (90) days' written notice, subject to a wind-down transition period not to exceed forty-five (45) days.
