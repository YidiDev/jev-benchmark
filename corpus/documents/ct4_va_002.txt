INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: Finance and Procurement Staff
From: Daniel Ruiz, Contracts Administrator
Date: May 6, 2024
Re: Confirmation of Active Payroll Processing Agreement

This is to confirm that the Payroll Processing Services Agreement with Kestrel Payroll Solutions, signed on October 4, 2023, remains the current and fully active agreement governing our outsourced payroll operations.

Kestrel continues to process biweekly payroll runs, tax filings, and year-end reporting for all three of our office locations under the terms established in the original agreement. There have been no changes to the fee structure, service levels, or points of contact since the agreement took effect.

Staff should continue submitting payroll adjustment requests through the Kestrel portal by the standard Wednesday deadline, and any escalations should still be routed to their account manager, Lisa Farron, at the contact information on file.

Please retain this memo for your records as confirmation that this agreement remains the governing document for our payroll relationship with Kestrel.

Daniel Ruiz
Contracts Administrator
