TO: Department Heads, Northgate Regional Hospital
FROM: Priya Anand, Director of Procurement and Vendor Relations
DATE: September 3, 2024
RE: Executed Service Agreement – Cascade Linen & Uniform Supply

Colleagues,

I'm circulating this memo to keep everyone informed of an administrative matter that will affect several departments in the coming months, and I want to begin by giving a bit of background on how Northgate Regional Hospital approaches vendor relationships more broadly, since this seems to come up often in new-employee orientation sessions.

Northgate Regional Hospital has operated in this community since 1962, when it began as a 40-bed facility serving primarily rural patients from the surrounding county. Over the decades, we have expanded to a 210-bed regional medical center with specialty units in cardiology, oncology, and neonatal care. Our procurement department manages relationships with over 300 active vendors across categories ranging from medical supplies to facilities maintenance, and we pride ourselves on maintaining rigorous vetting standards for every contracted partner, consistent with our mission statement of "compassionate care through operational excellence."

As part of our ongoing effort to streamline linen and uniform services across all departments, I am pleased to report that Northgate Regional Hospital has finalized and executed a service agreement with Cascade Linen & Uniform Supply, effective October 1, 2024. The agreement was signed on August 28, 2024 by David Okonkwo, Chief Operating Officer of Northgate Regional Hospital, and by Teresa Vance, President of Cascade Linen & Uniform Supply.

Under the terms of the agreement, Cascade Linen & Uniform Supply will provide weekly laundering, delivery, and replacement of scrubs, lab coats, and patient linens for all inpatient units, at a rate of $18,400 per month for a term of 24 months, with automatic renewal absent written notice of termination at least 60 days prior to expiration. Cascade has agreed to maintain a 99.5% on-time delivery standard and to carry liability insurance coverage of no less than $2,000,000 per occurrence. Northgate Regional Hospital, in turn, is obligated to provide a dedicated loading dock access window between 5:00 a.m. and 7:00 a.m. daily and to designate a single point of contact for inventory reconciliation, a role that will be filled by Facilities Coordinator Ben Truong.

I want to take this opportunity to remind all staff of our standard confidentiality policy regarding vendor contracts: the terms, pricing, and specific obligations outlined in vendor agreements are considered internal and proprietary and should not be shared outside the organization without prior authorization from the Procurement Office. This applies equally to pricing information, service-level terms, and any performance metrics discussed in vendor review meetings.

For context on why this vendor was selected, our evaluation committee reviewed proposals from four linen service providers over a six-week period earlier this year, weighing factors including cost, delivery reliability, environmental sustainability practices, and references from comparable regional hospitals. Cascade Linen & Uniform Supply, based in nearby Millbrook, has served three other regional hospitals in our network since 2011 and came highly recommended by our colleagues at Fairview Medical Center.

As a reminder, Northgate's broader sustainability initiative, launched in 2019, continues to guide many of our vendor decisions, and Cascade's use of water-reclamation laundering technology was a contributing factor in this selection.

Please direct any operational questions about the transition to Ben Truong, and any contractual questions to my office directly.

Regards,
Priya Anand
Director of Procurement and Vendor Relations
Northgate Regional Hospital
