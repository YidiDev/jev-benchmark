Dear Mr. Okafor,

This letter confirms the terms of the Consulting Services Agreement executed between Thornbury & Associates Consulting ("Consultant") and Vantage Retail Holdings ("Client"), effective August 15, 2024.

Consultant agrees to provide supply chain optimization services, including quarterly performance audits and staff training sessions, for an initial term of six months, ending February 15, 2025. Client agrees to compensate Consultant a flat fee of $42,000, payable in two installments of $21,000 each, due at contract signing and at the three-month mark.

Consultant shall maintain professional liability coverage of at least $2,000,000 throughout the engagement term. Client shall provide reasonable access to relevant staff and records necessary to complete the work outlined in Schedule A, attached separately. Either party may terminate this agreement upon 30 days' written notice for cause.

This letter, once countersigned, serves as the binding agreement between our organizations.
