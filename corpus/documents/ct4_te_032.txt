ADMINISTRATIVE FILING NOTICE
Subject: Status Update - Payroll Processing Services Agreement

This filing documents the current status of the Payroll Processing Services Agreement between Thornfield Manufacturing Corp. and Ledger Point Solutions Inc., originally dated May 15, 2020.

This Agreement was superseded by the Amended and Restated Agreement dated February 8, 2022, which revises the per-employee processing fee, adds provisions for quarterly tax filing support, and updates the data security requirements to reflect current compliance standards.

Effective February 8, 2022, the original Payroll Processing Services Agreement dated May 15, 2020 ceased to have any legal or operational effect. All parties and internal departments are instructed to rely solely on the terms of the February 8, 2022 Amended and Restated Agreement for matters concerning fees, deliverables, service standards, and dispute resolution procedures.

This notice is entered into the corporate contracts record for reference by Accounting, Human Resources, and Legal Affairs. Inquiries regarding the amended terms should be directed to the Vendor Contracts desk.
