Dear Mr. Achebe,

I am writing on behalf of Hallworth & Prescott Business Park Management to formally confirm the terms of our newly established service arrangement, and to share some general context regarding our management company's operations for your records.

Hallworth & Prescott has managed commercial office and retail properties throughout the greater Charlotte metropolitan area since 1979. Our current portfolio includes fourteen properties totaling approximately 1.2 million square feet of leasable space, serving a mix of corporate tenants, retail operators, and small professional offices. Over the decades, our approach to grounds and exterior maintenance has evolved considerably, particularly as our properties have grown in both number and geographic spread across Mecklenburg County. We share this background simply to provide context for the scope of the relationship we are establishing with your firm.

Before proceeding to the specific terms of our agreement, I would like to note our standard confidentiality expectations regarding this correspondence. The pricing, scheduling, and service details discussed herein are intended solely for the parties to this agreement and should not be disclosed to competing landscaping firms or unrelated third parties without prior written consent. This is consistent with our general practice regarding all vendor correspondence, and we trust Achebe Landscape Design Group will treat this letter accordingly.

With that context established, this letter confirms that Hallworth & Prescott Business Park Management and Achebe Landscape Design Group have entered into a signed Landscape Maintenance Services Agreement, executed by both parties on September 12, 2024, with services commencing October 1, 2024. Under the terms of this agreement, Achebe Landscape Design Group shall provide weekly mowing and grounds upkeep, seasonal planting and mulching services, and snow removal support during winter months, across all fourteen properties in our portfolio. The agreement establishes an initial term of twenty-four months, with a total annual contract value of $196,800, payable in equal monthly installments of $16,400. Achebe Landscape Design Group agrees to maintain commercial general liability insurance of no less than $1,500,000 per occurrence, and to provide crews equipped with company-branded vehicles and identification badges for all on-site personnel. Hallworth & Prescott, for its part, agrees to designate a single point of contact at each property for scheduling coordination and to remit payment within twenty-one days of invoice receipt.

As a matter of general interest, our firm has worked with four different landscaping vendors over the past fifteen years, and this transition reflects our ongoing effort to consolidate services under a single, reliable provider capable of servicing our full portfolio consistently. We were pleased to learn that Achebe Landscape Design Group has served the Charlotte area since 2011 and currently maintains contracts with two other commercial property groups in the region, which speaks favorably to your firm's capacity and reputation.

Enclosed with this letter, for your general reference, please find a brief overview of our tenant communication procedures, which may be useful background as your crews begin coordinating site access with individual property managers. This enclosure is provided purely for informational convenience and does not modify the terms of the agreement described above.

We look forward to a productive working relationship and are confident that this arrangement will serve both our tenants and your firm well over the coming years. Please do not hesitate to contact my office directly should any questions arise regarding scheduling, invoicing, or site access during the initial transition period.

Sincerely,
