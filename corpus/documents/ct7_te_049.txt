To: Ms. Adrienne Cole, Director of Operations

Dear Ms. Cole,

Please accept this letter as our biweekly status update covering the period of September 16 through September 30. Filed by the Falcon team, this report summarizes progress on the customer onboarding redesign initiative.

Over the past two weeks, we completed the revised intake questionnaire, reduced average form completion time from eleven minutes to six, and resolved fourteen outstanding tickets related to duplicate account creation. User testing with eight external volunteers produced generally favorable feedback, though two participants noted confusion around the document upload step, which we have flagged for revision.

Looking ahead, we intend to finalize the upload workflow by October 11 and begin a limited rollout to twenty pilot accounts the following week. We do not anticipate any blockers at this time, though we would appreciate additional design review bandwidth if it becomes available.

Thank you for your continued support. We remain available to discuss any of the above in further detail.

Sincerely,

Marcus Whitfield
