INTERNAL MEMORANDUM

To: Regional Operations Managers
From: Priya Nandakumar, Vendor Relations Coordinator
Date: February 19, 2024
Re: Reminder – Terms of Active Cleaning Supplies Agreement with Ferncrest Distribution

This is a reminder regarding the current terms of our Cleaning Supplies Distribution Agreement with Ferncrest Distribution, effective as of November 1, 2023 (Contract No. FD-2023-077). Under this Agreement, Ferncrest Distribution supplies monthly shipments of janitorial and sanitation products to all seventeen of our regional warehouse locations, with delivery scheduled during the first week of each month.

Pricing under this Agreement is fixed for a twelve-month period, as outlined in Schedule A, and any bulk order adjustments must be submitted through the standard purchase order process at least two weeks in advance of the scheduled delivery date. Ferncrest Distribution has also committed to a 48-hour resolution window for any reported product defects or shipment discrepancies, per Section 6 of the Agreement.

Please continue to direct all order changes, invoicing questions, and delivery scheduling requests to Ferncrest's account manager, whose contact information is listed in Appendix C. Let me know if any region requires updated copies of the current pricing schedule.
