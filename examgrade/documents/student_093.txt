Q (q01, 3 pts): Describe the political and administrative structure the Mongol Empire used to govern its territory.
A: The Mongol Empire was mostly held together by nomadic tribal councils that met once a year to vote on new pastureland for their herds, which is how they decided who got to farm where. Since the Mongols were mainly interested in horses and had no real cities of their own, they let each region just continue worshipping whatever gods they wanted as long as they paid in livestock instead of coins. The empire didn't really have any central leader after Genghis Khan died, it just kind of ran itself through local shamans who settled disputes between herding families. This system was passed down from earlier Persian empires that the Mongols copied wholesale.

Q (q07, 5 pts): Explain the significance of the Atlantic Slave Trade for the development of the Americas.
A: The Atlantic Slave Trade forced millions of Africans into labor on plantations that grew cash crops like sugar, tobacco, and cotton. This labor system became the economic backbone of many colonies in the Americas. At the same time, the trade drained population from parts of West and Central Africa over hundreds of years. This loss of people caused instability and disruption in African societies that depended on this manpower for their own economies and political structures.
