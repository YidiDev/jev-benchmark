Q (q04, 3 pts): Explain the impact of the Black Death on European society and economy.
A: So the Black Death killed a ton of people obviously, and because of that there weren't enough workers left to do all the farming and stuff that needed doing. So the workers that were left could basically demand higher wages because they were in short supply, which is kind of ironic when you think about it, like the plague actually helped some peasants economically. And because of that the whole manor system where lords controlled serfs started falling apart, since serfs had more bargaining power now and could leave for better opportunities. This basically sped up the end of feudalism in Europe because the labor shortage broke the old system where lords had all the power over the peasants.

Q (q11, 3 pts): Explain the causes of the Scientific Revolution in Europe.
A: The Scientific Revolution involved a shift away from relying only on classical texts like those of Aristotle and religious doctrine as the ultimate sources of truth about the natural world.
