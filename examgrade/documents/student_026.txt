Q (q03, 3 pts): Describe the circumstances of the fall of the Byzantine Empire in 1453.
A: The Byzantine Empire fell in 1453 when the Ottoman Empire, led by Mehmed II, captured Constantinople. This ended the Roman imperial line that had technically continued since antiquity. It also gave the Ottomans control over important trade routes in the Eastern Mediterranean. Because of this, European powers were pushed to look for alternative routes to Asia, which ties into the later Age of Exploration.

Q (q04, 3 pts): Explain the impact of the Black Death on European society and economy.
A: The Black Death killed about a third of Europe's population in the mid-1300s. Because so many workers died, there was a labor shortage afterward. This shortage drove up wages for the surviving peasants and workers. It also weakened the manorial system and serfdom, since lords had less leverage over labor, which helped accelerate the decline of feudalism in Europe.

Q (q17, 3 pts): Describe the effects of the Opium Wars on China.
A: As a result of the Opium Wars, China was forced into signing unequal treaties with foreign powers. The Treaty of Nanjing was one of these. Under these agreements China ceded Hong Kong to Britain. Treaty ports were also opened, giving foreign powers privileged access and control over Chinese trade.
