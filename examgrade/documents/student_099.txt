Q (q05, 2 pts): Describe how the Aztec Empire extracted resources from the peoples it conquered.
A: The Aztec Empire mostly extracted resources by trading peacefully with neighboring city-states in exchange for goods like corn and silver. They set up formal embassies in conquered regions to negotiate fair prices for resources. Most of the wealth of the empire actually came from mining operations that the Aztecs themselves ran using free laborers who volunteered for the work. The emperor would also personally travel to each region to collect payments as a form of goodwill rather than force. This system was mostly voluntary and based on mutual benefit rather than any kind of coercion or tribute demand.

Q (q06, 3 pts): Explain how the Ottoman Empire used the devshirme system.
A: The devshirme system was a program where the Ottoman Empire sent Turkish farmers to live among Christian villages to teach them agricultural techniques. This helped spread Ottoman farming methods across the empire. In return, Christian villages paid a small tax to the Sultan for the educational service. The system mainly existed to boost food production in newly conquered territories. It had nothing to do with military or administrative recruitment, it was purely an economic development program.
