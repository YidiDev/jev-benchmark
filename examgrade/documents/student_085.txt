I'm not totally sure but I believe that made really long distance trade way more reliable over time. I also think, though I could be wrong on details, that Islam spread along the network through merchants and travelers, especially along the East African coast and into Southeast Asia, just from people interacting and trading with each other over generations. So overall I'd say those are the two things I feel fairly confident about.

Q (q12, 4 pts): Explain how Enlightenment political philosophy influenced later revolutions.
A: Enlightenment philosophy introduced key political concepts like natural rights, the social contract, and separation of powers. These ideas gave people a new framework for thinking about government and legitimacy. In the American Revolution, colonists used these concepts to argue for the right to self-government and independence from Britain. In the French Revolution, similar ideas fueled demands for liberty and equality and helped justify ending the absolute monarchy in France.
