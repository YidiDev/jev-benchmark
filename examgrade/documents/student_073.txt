A: The Black Death had major effects on Europe. It killed around a third of the population in the mid-1300s. Because so many workers died, there was a labor shortage afterward. This drove wages up for the survivors and weakened the manorial system, since lords needed workers and had less leverage over them. This helped speed along the decline of feudalism in Europe.
