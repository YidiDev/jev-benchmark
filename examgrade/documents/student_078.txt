Q (q01, 3 pts): Describe the political and administrative structure the Mongol Empire used to govern its territory.
A: So the Mongols, I think, ran their empire mostly through like one big central government that was basically the same everywhere, kind of like how China ran things with emperors and bureaucrats copying that whole system. I think Genghis Khan just put his sons in charge of provinces and they all reported back to one single capital that controlled taxes and laws for the whole empire the same way in every region. It was sort of like a feudal system in Europe where lords owed loyalty to a king, so the Mongols just borrowed that idea and used knights basically. Honestly the empire was really just held together by trade routes and armies moving around, and I think the administration was pretty much copied from the Romans in terms of roads and law codes. So overall the Mongols governed through a strict centralized bureaucracy modeled on Confucian exams administered in every territory equally.
