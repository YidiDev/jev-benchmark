Q (q09, 4 pts): Describe the Columbian Exchange and its effects.
A: The Columbian Exchange had major effects on both sides of the Atlantic. One huge impact was that Old World diseases like smallpox spread to the Americas and devastated indigenous populations, since they had no prior immunity to these illnesses. On the other side, New World crops like maize and potatoes were brought back to Afro-Eurasia, which boosted food supplies and caused population growth there. Additionally, European livestock and crops introduced into the Americas transformed the ecosystems and diets of American societies, reshaping how people lived on both sides of the ocean.
