Q (q04, 3 pts): Explain the impact of the Black Death on European society and economy.
A: One major impact of the Black Death was economic. So many people died that there was suddenly a shortage of laborers, and this gave surviving peasants more bargaining power, driving up wages and weakening the manorial system tied to serfdom. This helped speed up the decline of feudalism in Europe. Another impact was religious and social. When prayer and religious devotion did not stop the disease from spreading, people began to question the Church's authority and power, and this crisis of faith led to scapegoating of minority groups who were blamed for causing the plague.

Q (q12, 4 pts): Explain how Enlightenment political philosophy influenced later revolutions.
A: Enlightenment thinkers introduced ideas like natural rights, the social contract, and separation of powers into political thought. These concepts directly shaped the American Revolution, since colonists used arguments about natural rights and self-government to justify breaking away from Britain. The same ideas also influenced the French Revolution, where revolutionaries called for liberty and equality and sought to end the absolute power of the monarchy. In both cases, Enlightenment political philosophy gave people a framework to challenge existing authority and demand new forms of government.
