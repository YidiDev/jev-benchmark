Q (q03, 3 pts): Describe the circumstances of the fall of the Byzantine Empire in 1453.
A: The fall of the Byzantine Empire marked the end of the old Roman imperial line, since Byzantium had been seen as the continuation of Rome. Because of this, European powers were spurred to look for new routes to Asia, which eventually helped lead to the Age of Exploration.

Q (q04, 3 pts): Explain the impact of the Black Death on European society and economy.
A: The Black Death caused huge religious crises across Europe. People began scapegoating minority groups, blaming them for the plague. It also caused people to question the Church's authority, since prayer and religious rituals did not stop the disease from spreading or killing people.

Q (q13, 5 pts): Describe the causes and effects of the French Revolution.
A: The French Revolution was caused by resentment of the rigid estates system, since the nobility and clergy enjoyed privileges that the common people did not have. It was also caused by Enlightenment ideas about liberty, equality, and popular sovereignty, which inspired people to challenge the existing order. As a result, the monarchy was overthrown, and Louis XVI was eventually executed. The instability that followed the revolution eventually allowed Napoleon Bonaparte to rise to power.

Q (q18, 3 pts): Explain the significance of the Meiji Restoration in Japan.
A: The Meiji Restoration ended the Tokugawa Shogunate and formally restored imperial rule in Japan. It also led to Japan rapidly modernizing its military, industry, and government institutions, largely by copying Western models of organization and technology.
