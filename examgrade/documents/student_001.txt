Q (q01, 3 pts): Describe the political and administrative structure the Mongol Empire used to govern its territory.
A: The Mongols were able to control a massive empire made up of many different ethnic groups without relying on the old tribal or clan systems that existed before. This meant they could quickly organize and command huge numbers of people from very different backgrounds all at once, which let them mobilize armies and resources fast across their territory.

Q (q04, 3 pts): Explain the impact of the Black Death on European society and economy.
A: The Black Death killed about a third of Europe's population in the mid-1300s. This massive population loss created a labor shortage, which meant surviving workers could demand higher wages. This weakened the manorial system and serfdom, since lords had less leverage over peasants, and it helped speed up the decline of feudalism in Europe.
