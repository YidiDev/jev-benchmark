Q (q09, 4 pts): Describe the Columbian Exchange and its effects.
A: The Columbian Exchange refers to the transfer of crops, livestock, people, and diseases between the Americas and Afro-Eurasia starting after 1492. One major effect was that Old World diseases, like smallpox, devastated indigenous populations in the Americas because they had no prior immunity to these illnesses. On the other hand, New World crops such as maize and potatoes were brought to Afro-Eurasia, and these boosted food supplies there, which contributed to population growth in places like Europe and Asia. Overall this exchange reshaped populations and food systems across both hemispheres.

Q (q10, 3 pts): Explain the significance of the Tokugawa Shogunate's sakoku policy.

It limited who could enter and leave the country and controlled interactions with foreign merchants and missionaries.
