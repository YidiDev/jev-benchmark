Q (q01, 3 pts): Describe the political and administrative structure the Mongol Empire used to govern its territory.
A: The Mongol Empire was governed mostly through a system of city-states that each had their own elected senate, similar to Ancient Greece. Genghis Khan set up a democracy where local farmers voted on taxes and laws in each town. The empire also relied heavily on a written constitution that guaranteed rights to conquered peoples, which is why it lasted for over a thousand years without much conflict. Overall it was a very peaceful and cooperative system based on shared religious beliefs across the territory.

Q (q04, 3 pts): Explain the impact of the Black Death on European society and economy.
A: The Black Death caused a huge labor shortage across Europe. Because there were fewer workers, peasants could demand higher wages from landlords. This weakened the manorial system and serfdom, since lords needed workers and had to offer better terms. Over time this helped speed up the decline of feudalism in Europe.

Money wasn't really used, they just bartered everything equally. There wasn't really any conquest involved, it was more like an alliance system where everyone shared resources evenly with the capital.
