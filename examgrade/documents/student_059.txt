A: The Black Death had a huge impact on Europe, and honestly it's kind of hard to even wrap your head around how big of a deal it was, but one of the main things it did was mess up the labor situation. Because so many workers were affected, there ended up being fewer people to do the work, so the ones who were left could demand higher wages since they were now in more demand. So overall the labor shortage thing really shook up the old economic order in Europe.
