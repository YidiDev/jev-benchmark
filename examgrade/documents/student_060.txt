Q (q06, 3 pts): Explain how the Ottoman Empire used the devshirme system.
A: The devshirme system helped the Ottoman sultan build a group of soldiers and officials who owed loyalty only to him, not to old noble families. This meant the sultan had people he could really trust running the government and army, since they weren't tied to competing aristocratic bloodlines. It gave the central government more control over the empire.
