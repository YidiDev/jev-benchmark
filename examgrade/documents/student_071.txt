Q (q01, 3 pts): Describe the political and administrative structure the Mongol Empire used to govern its territory.
A: The Mongol Empire was founded by Genghis Khan. He brought the various Mongol groups together. This let the Mongols move fast and rule a huge multi-ethnic population. They didn't need to rely on the old tribal or clan hierarchies to do this. It just worked better for controlling so many different peoples at once.

Q (q02, 4 pts): Explain the significance of the Indian Ocean trade network before 1500 CE.
A: Okay so the Indian Ocean trade network before 1500, I keep thinking about how much stuff was moving around, like spices and textiles and porcelain and gold, all these goods going back and forth constantly. And a big part of why this worked so well is the monsoon winds, they reversed seasonally in a predictable way so sailors could actually plan when to sail out and when to come back, which is kind of the whole reason the trade could be so consistent over centuries. Also along with all the goods, Islam spread through this network too, merchants and travelers carried it along the East African coast and into Southeast Asia, so it wasn't just objects moving but religion and ideas as well. Yeah so goods, winds, and religion spreading, that's basically what made this trade network so significant.

Q (q03, 3 pts): Describe the circumstances of the fall of the Byzantine Empire in 1453.
A: The Ottoman Empire, led by Mehmed II, captured Constantinople in 1453. This ended the line of Roman emperors that had continued through the Byzantine Empire for centuries. It also gave the Ottomans control over key trade routes in the Eastern Mediterranean. Because of this, Europeans were pushed to look for new routes to Asia instead.

Q (q06, 3 pts): Explain how the Ottoman Empire used the devshirme system.
A: The devshirme system was a periodic levy where the Ottomans took Christian boys from provinces in the Balkans. This gave the sultan a way to build up a military and administrative class that was loyal directly to him instead of to hereditary nobles. It helped centralize power in the sultan's hands rather than leaving it with old noble families.

Q (q07, 5 pts): Explain the significance of the Atlantic Slave Trade for the development of the Americas.
A: The Atlantic Slave Trade connected Europe, West Africa, and the Americas in a triangular trade system, forcibly transporting millions of enslaved Africans across the ocean. Once in the Americas, these enslaved people were forced to work on plantations growing cash crops like sugar, tobacco, and cotton. Over several centuries this trade depopulated and destabilized parts of West and Central Africa. It also left a lasting African cultural, religious, and linguistic influence throughout the Americas that is still visible today.

A: Martin Luther posted his Ninety-Five Theses in 1517. He was criticizing the Catholic Church. He objected to indulgences being sold. He also saw the Church as corrupt. These grievances sparked the Reformation.
