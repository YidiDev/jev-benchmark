Q (q01, 3 pts): Describe the political and administrative structure the Mongol Empire used to govern its territory.
A: Mongols organized people by units of ten. Ten, hundred, thousand, ten-thousand. Cut across old tribal lines. Broke up old clan loyalty. Let them mobilize huge armies fast. Controlled many different peoples without needing old tribal chiefs. Simple system. Worked well for war and control.

Q (q03, 3 pts): Describe the circumstances of the fall of the Byzantine Empire in 1453.
A: The Byzantine Empire fell in 1453 when the Ottoman Empire, led by Mehmed II, captured the capital city of Constantinople. This ended the long line of Roman imperial rule that had continued through Byzantium for centuries. It also gave the Ottomans control over important trade routes in the Eastern Mediterranean. This loss of easy access pushed European powers to look for new sea routes to Asia.

Q (q06, 3 pts): Explain how the Ottoman Empire used the devshirme system.
A: The devshirme system involved the Ottomans periodically taking Christian boys from the Balkan provinces. These boys were then converted to Islam and put through training. Many of them ended up becoming elite Janissary soldiers, while others rose to become high-ranking administrators in the empire.

Q (q08, 3 pts): Explain the causes of the Protestant Reformation.
A: The Protestant Reformation began largely because of Martin Luther, who in 1517 wrote the Ninety-Five Theses criticizing the Catholic Church. A major point of his criticism was the sale of indulgences, along with what he and others saw as widespread corruption within the Church. These grievances sparked a broader movement against Catholic authority.

Q (q09, 4 pts): Describe the Columbian Exchange and its effects.
A: The Columbian Exchange refers to the transfer of crops, livestock, people, and diseases between the Americas and Afro-Eurasia after 1492. One major effect was that New World crops like maize and potatoes reached Afro-Eurasia and helped boost food supplies there. This contributed to population growth across Europe, Asia, and Africa in the following centuries.

Q (q12, 4 pts): Explain how Enlightenment political philosophy influenced later revolutions.
A: Enlightenment thinkers like Locke, Rousseau, and Montesquieu developed new political philosophies about government and rights. These ideas directly shaped the American Revolution, which used arguments about self-government to justify breaking from British rule. The same ideas also directly influenced the French Revolution, where calls for liberty, equality, and ending absolute monarchy became central demands.

Q (q13, 5 pts): Describe the causes and effects of the French Revolution.
A: The French Revolution had several causes. One was the chronic debt of the French government, made worse by expensive wars it had fought. Another was widespread resentment of the rigid estates system, which gave nobles and clergy privileges that commoners didn't have. In the aftermath, the political instability that followed the revolution eventually allowed Napoleon Bonaparte to rise to power.

Q (q17, 3 pts): Describe the effects of the Opium Wars on China.
A: The Opium Wars ended badly for China. China was militarily defeated by Britain, and later other powers joined in as well. As a result, China was forced into unequal treaties, like the Treaty of Nanjing. These treaties made China cede Hong Kong and open treaty ports to foreign control.

Q (q27, 4 pts): Describe the causes and effects of the fall of the Soviet Union.
A: The fall of the Soviet Union had a few key causes. One was long-term economic stagnation, since the centrally planned economy had become inefficient over time. Another was the rise of nationalist movements within the various Soviet republics, which pushed hard for independence from Moscow. In the end, the collapse ended the Cold War and brought an end to the bipolar global power structure that had defined international politics for decades.
