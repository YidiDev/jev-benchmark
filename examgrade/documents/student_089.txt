Q (q01, 3 pts): Describe the political and administrative structure the Mongol Empire used to govern its territory.
A: The Mongol Empire was founded by Genghis Khan. It ruled a huge multi-ethnic population. This let them mobilize armies and command people fast. They didn't need old tribal or clan systems to run things. It worked across many different groups.
