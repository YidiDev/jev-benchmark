Q (q01, 3 pts): Describe the political and administrative structure the Mongol Empire used to govern its territory.
A: The Mongols organized people into units. Groups of 10. Groups of 100. Groups of 1,000. Groups of 10,000. This cut across old tribal lines. It was a military system mostly. People served in these units.

Q (q02, 4 pts): Explain the significance of the Indian Ocean trade network before 1500 CE.
A: The Indian Ocean trade network moved goods like spices, textiles, porcelain, and gold across long distances. Sailors relied on the monsoon winds, which reversed direction seasonally, so they could plan trips out and back with some predictability. This trade also helped spread Islam along the East African coast and into Southeast Asia, as merchants and travelers brought the religion with them as they moved through these networks.

Q (q03, 3 pts): Describe the circumstances of the fall of the Byzantine Empire in 1453.
A: Byzantine Empire fell. It ended the Roman line. This ended old Roman rule. Ottomans took over trade routes. Europe had to find new paths to Asia because of this. That's the main effect.

Q (q04, 3 pts): Explain the impact of the Black Death on European society and economy.
A: The Black Death caused a labor shortage in Europe. This shortage drove up wages for workers. It also weakened the manorial and serfdom system that had existed before. Because of this, feudalism began to decline faster than it would have otherwise.

Q (q05, 2 pts): Describe how the Aztec Empire extracted resources from the peoples it conquered.
A: The Aztec Empire used a tribute system where conquered city-states had to regularly send goods, labor, or soldiers to the empire. This meant conquered peoples were expected to keep providing resources on an ongoing basis rather than a one time thing.

Q (q07, 5 pts): Explain the significance of the Atlantic Slave Trade for the development of the Americas.
A: I think the Atlantic Slave Trade was really significant for the Americas mainly because enslaved Africans were forced to work on plantations, growing cash crops like sugar, tobacco, and cotton. That plantation labor produced huge profits, and I believe this wealth is part of what fueled European trading empires and their colonial economies for a long time. I also think, over the centuries, this trade depopulated and destabilized parts of West and Central Africa since so many people were taken away. And I'm fairly confident it also left a lasting mark of African culture, religion, and language throughout the Americas, things like music and food traditions that are still around today.

Q (q16, 4 pts): Explain the causes of European imperialism in Africa in the late 19th century.
A: European imperialism in Africa was driven by competition and prestige rivalry among European powers, which was formalized through events like the Berlin Conference. Ideologies like Social Darwinism and racial theories were also used to justify domination, framing it as a civilizing mission for supposedly inferior peoples. Additionally, military technology such as the Maxim gun gave European powers a decisive advantage over African societies, making conquest easier.
