This caused the king to raise taxes only on the clergy, which made the church rebel against the monarchy. As a result, the monarchy was replaced by a military government led by Robespierre, who then became emperor and expanded France's borders peacefully through trade agreements. The effects included increased friendship between France and Britain and a rise in agricultural production due to new machinery invented during the revolution.
