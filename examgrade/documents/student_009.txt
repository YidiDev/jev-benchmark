Q (q01, 3 pts): Describe the political and administrative structure the Mongol Empire used to govern its territory.
A: The Mongol Empire was able to run such a massive territory because it didn't just rely on old tribal or clan loyalties to hold things together. Instead they built a system that let them quickly mobilize and command a huge population made up of tons of different ethnic groups. This meant they weren't limited by whichever clan someone happened to be born into, which helped them control so much land so fast.

Q (q03, 3 pts): Describe the circumstances of the fall of the Byzantine Empire in 1453.
A: In 1453 the Ottoman Empire, led by Mehmed II, conquered Constantinople and this finally brought down the Byzantine Empire. This is significant because it ended the line of the Roman Empire that had technically been continuing there for centuries. It also gave the Ottomans control over key trade routes in the Eastern Mediterranean, which pushed European powers to start looking for alternate routes to Asia since the old ones were now controlled by the Ottomans.

Q (q04, 3 pts): Explain the impact of the Black Death on European society and economy.
A: The Black Death caused a huge religious crisis in Europe. People prayed and prayed but the plague kept killing people anyway, so many started to lose faith in the Church's power and authority. This also led to scapegoating, where minority groups like Jewish communities were blamed for causing the plague and faced persecution as a result.
