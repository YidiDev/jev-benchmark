Q (q02, 4 pts): Explain the significance of the Indian Ocean trade network before 1500 CE.
A: The Indian Ocean trade network was significant because of the wide variety of valuable goods that moved through it, including spices, textiles, porcelain, and gold. These goods connected a huge range of regions across the ocean basin. Specifically, the network linked East Africa along the Swahili coast, Arabia, India, Southeast Asia, and China into one large interconnected trading system. This shows how these different regions were economically tied together well before 1500.

Q (q15, 4 pts): Explain the process of industrialization in Britain.

As industry grew, there was rapid urbanization, with workers moving into industrial cities to find factory work, often living and working under harsh conditions.
