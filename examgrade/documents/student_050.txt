Q (q09, 4 pts): Describe the Columbian Exchange and its effects.
A: The Columbian Exchange had several major effects. One was that Old World diseases like smallpox spread to the Americas and devastated indigenous populations, since they had no prior immunity to these illnesses. Another effect was that New World crops such as maize and potatoes were brought to Afro-Eurasia, and this boosted food supplies and helped populations grow there. Finally, European livestock and crops were introduced to the Americas, which transformed American ecosystems and diets and reshaped societies on both sides of the Atlantic. These effects together show how interconnected the two hemispheres became after 1492.
