Q (q15, 4 pts): Explain the process of industrialization in Britain.
A: Britain's industrialization involved several key developments. First, the steam engine was crucial because it powered machinery in factories and later powered railways and ships, allowing much faster production and transportation. Second, there was a shift away from home-based cottage industry, where people made goods in their own houses, toward centralized factories where production could be organized on a much larger scale. These two changes together transformed how goods were made and moved throughout Britain.
