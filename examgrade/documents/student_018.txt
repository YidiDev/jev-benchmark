Q (q01, 3 pts): Describe the political and administrative structure the Mongol Empire used to govern its territory.
A: The Mongols organized their people using a decimal system, breaking society into units of 10, 100, 1,000, and 10,000 soldiers/families. This system cut across the old tribal and clan loyalties that had divided the steppe peoples before. It was a very organized and structured way of grouping people for military purposes.

Q (q02, 4 pts): Explain the significance of the Indian Ocean trade network before 1500 CE.
A: The Indian Ocean trade network relied heavily on the monsoon winds, which blew in different directions depending on the season. Sailors could predict when the winds would reverse, which let them plan their voyages out and back at reliable times of year. This predictability made long-distance sea travel across the Indian Ocean much more reliable than it might otherwise have been. Knowing the wind patterns was key to making these trips work.

Q (q03, 3 pts): Describe the circumstances of the fall of the Byzantine Empire in 1453.
A: So basically in 1453 the Ottoman Empire, led by Mehmed II, finally captured Constantinople after a long siege, which is a huge deal because it ends the Roman Empire's imperial line once and for all, like the last little piece of Rome that had survived for a thousand years after the west fell. And with the Ottomans now controlling Constantinople they had control over these really important trade routes in the Eastern Mediterranean that connected Europe to Asia. So this pushed European powers, since they couldn't rely on those routes as easily anymore, to start looking for new routes to Asia, like sailing around Africa or west across the Atlantic, which ties into the Age of Exploration later. So yeah the fall of Constantinople was this huge turning point, ending Rome and reshuffling trade and pushing Europe to explore, it's kind of circling back to the same idea of trade routes shifting because of Ottoman control.

Q (q05, 2 pts): Describe how the Aztec Empire extracted resources from the peoples it conquered.
A: The Aztec Empire relied on a tribute system, where conquered city-states had to regularly send goods, labor, or soldiers to the empire. This meant subject peoples were not directly ruled day to day but still had to provide resources on a set schedule.

Q (q06, 3 pts): Explain how the Ottoman Empire used the devshirme system.
A: The devshirme system took young boys and converted them to Islam, then trained them for years. Many of these boys ended up becoming Janissary soldiers, who were elite fighters, or they rose up to become important administrators in the government.

Q (q09, 4 pts): Describe the Columbian Exchange and its effects.
A: One major effect of the Columbian Exchange was the spread of diseases like smallpox from the Old World to the New World. Indigenous peoples in the Americas had no prior immunity to these diseases. As a result, native populations were devastated by these illnesses after contact with Europeans.
