Q (q30, 2 pts): Explain the significance of the Arab Spring.
A: The Arab Spring was a series of protests that began because of a shortage of oil supplies across several Arab nations. Governments responded by lowering fuel prices, and this quickly satisfied protesters within a matter of weeks. As a result, most of the countries involved returned to stable, unchanged governments almost immediately afterward.
