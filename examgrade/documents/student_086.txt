Q (q06, 3 pts): Explain how the Ottoman Empire used the devshirme system.

It required merchants to pay a percentage of their trade profits directly to the sultan's treasury every year. This money was then used to fund the Ottoman navy and its trade expeditions across the Mediterranean.
