Q (q02, 4 pts): Explain the significance of the Indian Ocean trade network before 1500 CE.
A: The Indian Ocean trade network relied heavily on the monsoon winds, which blew in one direction for part of the year and reversed direction for the other part. This predictability meant sailors could time their voyages to sail out and then come back later when the winds switched, making long round trips possible without having to guess when it was safe to travel. This was a huge deal because it let merchants plan ahead in a way that wasn't really possible in other trade systems that depended on unpredictable weather. Overall this wind pattern was one of the main reasons the network could function as reliably as it did over such a long period of time.
