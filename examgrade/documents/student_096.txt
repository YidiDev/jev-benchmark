Q (q14, 4 pts): Explain the significance of the Haitian Revolution.
A: The Haitian Revolution was significant because it was the only successful large-scale slave revolt in history, and it led directly to the creation of an independent nation, Haiti. It also had a broader impact because it inspired enslaved and colonized people in other parts of the world to resist their own oppressors, while at the same time it deeply alarmed other slaveholding powers who feared similar uprisings.
