Q (q15, 4 pts): Explain the process of industrialization in Britain.
A: Britain's industrialization involved a shift away from cottage industry, where people made goods at home, toward centralized factories where production was concentrated in one place. This was possible partly because Britain had abundant domestic coal and iron deposits, which provided the fuel and raw materials needed for industrial machinery. As factories grew, there was also rapid urbanization, with workers moving into industrial cities, often living and working under pretty harsh conditions.
