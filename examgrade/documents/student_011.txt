Q (q01, 3 pts): Describe the political and administrative structure the Mongol Empire used to govern its territory.
A: Mongols organized people into units of 10, 100, 1,000, 10,000. This cut across old tribal lines. Old clan loyalty didn't matter anymore. This let them mobilize huge armies fast. Many different ethnic groups could be commanded together. No need for old tribal hierarchy at all.

Q (q06, 3 pts): Explain how the Ottoman Empire used the devshirme system.
A: I think the devshirme system was basically a periodic collection, like a levy, of Christian boys from the Balkan provinces of the empire. From what I remember, these boys were then converted to Islam and given training, and a lot of them ended up becoming Janissary soldiers, who were like an elite military corps, or sometimes they rose up into high administrative positions in the government. I believe the real significance of this was that it gave the Sultan a military and administrative class that was loyal directly to him personally, rather than to some hereditary noble family, so it helped centralize power around the Sultan instead of letting old aristocratic families build up independent influence.
