Q (q05, 2 pts): Describe how the Aztec Empire extracted resources from the peoples it conquered.
A: The Aztec Empire mainly extracted resources through a tribute system. Conquered city-states were required to regularly send goods, labor, or military service to the empire as a form of submission. This system allowed the Aztecs to fund their imperial capital, Tenochtitlan, and support the elite class without having to directly administer every single conquered territory themselves. Basically it let local rulers stay in charge locally as long as they kept paying tribute, which made controlling such a large empire more efficient.

A: The Columbian Exchange caused big changes across the world. Old World diseases like smallpox spread to the Americas and devastated indigenous populations, since they had no prior immunity to these illnesses. This led to massive population loss in the Americas. On the other side, New World crops like maize and potatoes were brought to Afro-Eurasia. These crops boosted food supplies there and contributed to population growth across Europe, Asia, and Africa.

However, it wasn't a complete shutdown, since limited Dutch trade was still allowed through the controlled port of Nagasaki, specifically at Dejima. So Japan wasn't entirely cut off, but interaction with the outside world was very tightly controlled.
