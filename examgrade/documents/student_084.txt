Q (q01, 3 pts): Describe the political and administrative structure the Mongol Empire used to govern its territory.
A: The Mongol Empire was founded by Genghis Khan, who united the various steppe peoples under his rule. Because of the way he organized his empire, the Mongols were able to quickly mobilize and command a massive population made up of many different ethnic groups without having to depend on the old tribal or clan hierarchies that existed before. This meant loyalty was owed to Genghis Khan and his system rather than to older family or tribal loyalties.

Q (q02, 4 pts): Explain the significance of the Indian Ocean trade network before 1500 CE.
A: The Indian Ocean trade network was significant because it helped spread Islam along the East African coast and into Southeast Asia. Merchants and travelers who took part in the trade brought their religion with them as they moved between ports, and over time local populations in these coastal areas adopted Islam as a result of this contact.

A: The fall of the Byzantine Empire in 1453 happened because the attacking forces used massive siege cannons that were able to break through the city's very old and thick walls, which had protected it for centuries before that. These new weapons made it possible to finally breach defenses that had held for so long.

Q (q04, 3 pts): Explain the impact of the Black Death on European society and economy.
A: The Black Death had a huge impact on Europe because it killed around a third of the entire population in the mid-1300s. This was an enormous loss of life that happened over a fairly short period of time and affected nearly every part of the continent.

Q (q05, 2 pts): Describe how the Aztec Empire extracted resources from the peoples it conquered.
A: The Aztec Empire mainly got resources from conquered peoples through a tribute system. Conquered city-states were required to regularly send goods, labor, or soldiers to the Aztec rulers as a form of payment for being allowed to remain semi-autonomous.

Q (q06, 3 pts): Explain how the Ottoman Empire used the devshirme system.
A: Okay so the devshirme system, I keep thinking about what it actually did, and I think the main point of it was that it created this class of soldiers and administrators who were loyal directly to the Sultan instead of being loyal to some noble family or hereditary lord. Like, the whole point was cutting out the middleman of nobility, so the people in charge of the military and government owed everything to the Sultan personally. I guess that's the big picture thing, that it built up a group tied to the ruler himself rather than old bloodlines or noble houses, which made the empire more centralized around the Sultan's power.
