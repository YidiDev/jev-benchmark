Q (q01, 3 pts): Describe the political and administrative structure the Mongol Empire used to govern its territory.
A: The empire ran on a decimal system. People organized into groups of 10, 100, 1,000, 10,000. This cut across old tribal lines. Made it easy to command huge groups of different peoples. No need to rely on old clan chiefs. Very efficient for war and control.

Q (q06, 3 pts): Explain how the Ottoman Empire used the devshirme system.
A: The devshirme system involved the periodic levy of Christian boys from the Balkan provinces of the empire. These boys were taken and converted to Islam. They were then trained, and many became elite Janissary soldiers. Others rose to become high administrators within the Ottoman government.

Q (q13, 5 pts): Describe the causes and effects of the French Revolution.
A: The French Revolution had several major causes. First, the government faced chronic debt and a fiscal crisis made worse by costly wars, including support for the American Revolution. Second, there was deep resentment of the rigid estates system, since the nobility and clergy held privileges that the common people did not share. Third, Enlightenment ideas about liberty, equality, and popular sovereignty inspired people to question the old order. In terms of effects, the instability and chaos that followed the revolution eventually allowed Napoleon Bonaparte to rise to power and take control of France.

Q (q14, 4 pts): Explain the significance of the Haitian Revolution.
A: Toussaint Louverture led the uprising of the enslaved population in Saint-Domingue. This revolt was the only successful large-scale slave revolt in history that resulted in the founding of an independent nation, Haiti. As a result of the revolution, France lost its most profitable colony, which weakened its position and influence in the Americas.

A: I think the Chinese Communist Revolution had a few major causes worth mentioning. First, there was the ongoing struggle between the Chinese Communist Party, led by Mao Zedong, and the Nationalist Guomindang government under Chiang Kai-shek, who were rivals fighting for control of China. I believe the Communists gained a lot of support from the rural peasantry because they promised land reform, which appealed strongly to poor farmers who made up most of the population. I also think the Japanese invasion during World War II played a big role, since it significantly weakened the Nationalist government's forces and resources, which ended up benefiting the Communists and helping them gain strength relative to the Nationalists. So overall it seems like it was this combination of ideological rivalry, peasant appeal through land reform, and the weakening effect of the Japanese invasion that let the Communists eventually win.
